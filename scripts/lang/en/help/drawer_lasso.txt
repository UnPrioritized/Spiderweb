Draw a loop round part of your drawing and press Enter: every stroke the loop crosses is cut there, and the parts inside the loop are picked, so Delete takes them away. Esc throws the loop away. A stroke wholly inside the loop isn't cut, just picked.

The ▾ next to the Lasso button picks Lasso (drag round and let go: the loop closes straight back to the start and is smoothed into curves; Alt = straight lines) or Polygonal (click its corners; they stick to points and lines like when drawing, Shift = free; click the first point, double-click, right-click or press Enter to close; Backspace = last point off).

Closed strokes are cut too wherever the loop goes in and out of them. With strokes picked (Select tool), only those are cut; otherwise every stroke the loop crosses. Hidden and locked layers are never cut. With Mirror on, the mirrored loop cuts too.

While the loop waits you can drag its points, Alt-drag a point for curve handles, middle-click the loop to add a point and right-click a point to remove it. Ctrl+Z undoes those changes one at a time, then throws the loop away.

The pieces stay where they were, in the same layer and group. Curves, arcs and polylines stay curves, arcs and polylines; circles, freehand strokes and strokes with a Formula become polylines. Ctrl+Z undoes a cut.
