Spiderweb answers the mouse and the keys the same way in every window, so what works in one place works in the others too. Every key one by one: [page:shortcuts|Keyboard and mouse shortcuts].

## Things you grab

Points, handles, knobs and sliders, in a graph, on the piano roll or in the Hz bass window:

• Drag = change it.
• Right-click = a menu. It never deletes anything by itself: Delete point is in the menu (greyed for the first and last point of a graph: they stay).
• Double-click = straighten (a bent piece goes straight). It never deletes.
• Middle-click = back to its default (a knob to 0, a slider to its usual value).
• Shift while dragging = off the grid.

Clicks pair up: a double-click and then one more quick click is a double-click and a click, never two double-clicks. After you pick something from a right-click menu you can click again at once; only the click that closes a menu without picking anything does nothing.

Two places keep their own way on purpose: a Hz bass note double-clicked with the Pencil is deleted (like many MIDI programs' pencil), and right-clicking the first or last point of a note's bend opens the note's menu. The drawing tools' curves work like a pen tool and keep their own way too: right-click an anchor of a curve, a lasso or a formula's curve to take its handles off or remove it.

## Number boxes

Drag a box, or the name beside it, sideways; Up / Down in the box; the mouse wheel after a click in it. Shift = big steps, Ctrl = small steps. Two boxes that are a start and an end move together from the name over both or the ↔ between them. More in [page:numbers|Number boxes].

## Mouse wheel

Wheel = scroll, Shift+wheel = sideways, Ctrl+Shift+wheel = zoom time, Alt+wheel = zoom keys, Ctrl+Alt+wheel = zoom both. Ctrl+wheel = the next / previous track where there are tracks (piano roll, velocity and Conductor panes), a zoom where there are none (Hz bass window). A box, dropdown or knob changes on the wheel only after you click it.

## Tool windows

• OK keeps what you tried and closes the window. Cancel, Esc or the window's X put everything back.
• Windows where every change is already its own undo step (Stir, Warp, Liquify, Shares, Properties) have just Close.
• Enter = the window's main button (OK, Use it, Apply…), except while you type in a box: there Enter takes the box's number.
• F1 = this window's own Help page.
• Every control shows a short help when the mouse rests on it.
