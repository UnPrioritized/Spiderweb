Draw lines, curves and shapes on the piano roll; every key they cross gets notes, like drawing notes by hand but in one stroke.

1. Pick a tool at the top and draw. The ▾ arrow next to the drawing tool button ([page:line|Line] at first) opens every drawing tool ([page:poly|Polyline], [page:free|Freehand], [page:curve|Curve], [page:arc|Arc], [page:custom|Custom shape], [page:funnel|Funnel], [page:text|Text]…); the pin next to one gives it its own button.

2. Select a shape to change it: drag its points, or change its settings in the panel on the right.

3. Space plays it. Right-drag listens to the notes under the mouse.

4. Set PPQ and the output file under Project (the tempo goes in the [page:conductor|Conductor pane]), then [page:files|Export MIDI].

Your work is saved on its own (autosave) and comes back next time. File → Save keeps it as a project file.

The first time you use a tool or feature, a small tip explains it. You can turn tips off or show them all again in this Help window (bottom).
