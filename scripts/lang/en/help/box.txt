Drag a box on the piano roll, or click two corners. Ctrl makes it perfect as it looks on screen (a circle, a square, an equal-sided triangle or star).

The panel sets how many Points the polygon has (3 = triangle, 4 = square, 5 = pentagon…) and its Kind:

Polygon: a flat side at the bottom.

Star: outer and inner points take turns. Inner size is how far out the inner points are, in % of the outer ones (100 = a polygon with twice the points).

Crossing star: joins every Skip-th point with straight lines that cross, like a pentagram (5 points, skip 2). When the two share a divisor, it's several loops (6 points, skip 2 = two triangles).

Change them any time with the polygon selected; the panel's numbers are also used for the next one you draw.
[clip:polygon]

The sides can have a shape: right-click the polygon → Formula → Shape of the line (Quarter circle, S-curve, your own…). Every side becomes that shape, from its corner to the next one, so a square can become a flower of arches. A closed shape (like Circle) sits on each side as wide as the side, and the sides stay straight.

The sides can also have a pattern: right-click the polygon → Formula → Pattern along the line (a wave, zigzag, spikes, your own…). Every side gets the same pattern. Each piece = every side gets all the loops; Across all = the loops run on round the whole outline.

Its height is in hundredths of the polygon's height, so it grows with the shape. Symmetric halves work on it too. "Turn into plain lines" makes the result plain lines (the polygon settings go).

They're custom shapes, so their inside can be filled (see Inside fill) and they resize, turn and skew like any custom shape (see Custom shapes: resize, turn, skew). With Live shape on they're drawn into the live shape instead.
