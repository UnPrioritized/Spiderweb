The Shapes list (side panel, below Project) shows every shape on the piano roll, one row each: its number, its name, how many notes it makes and, with Multi channel, how many channels it spreads over. A row turns orange when its shape needs more channels than there are colours, so colours and channels repeat.

The list is in the order the shapes were made, the newest at the bottom. Further down = on top: where the insides of shapes overlap, a click picks the one on top. Masks in one group are worked through from the top of the list down (the oldest first).

Click a row to select that shape; Ctrl+click or Shift+click selects more. Selecting on the piano roll highlights the rows too.

Duplicate copies the selected shapes one snap step later (one beat with Snap off). Delete removes the selected shapes. Delete all asks first; Ctrl+Z brings them back either way.

Some rows say more: (Sustained), (Gates flipped) or (Subtracted) after the name, and an Add between group is one row (clicking it selects the whole group).
