The Deform row in the side panel (under Recolour) holds [page:stir|Stir…], [page:warp|Warp…] and [page:liquify|Liquify…]. Each of them can bend either the colours inside a shape or the shape itself. This page says how the two ways fit together; each tool's own page says how to use it.

## Colours or Shape

"Moves" at the top of each window picks what it bends. Colours (the start) moves only the colours: the notes stay where they are, and each note takes the colour the bend brings to its place. Shape bends the shape itself. In Stir each tab has its own choice; Warp and Liquify have one each per shape.

"Cut notes where bent" (under Moves, ticked at first) says how a drawn shape bends:

- Ticked: the shape's notes are made first, then each note is bent and cut where the bend curves it, the same way pasted notes bend (see below). Fill rows, Spam and patterns bend along like a picture.
- Unticked: only the outline bends, and the notes are laid out again inside the bent outline. A Fill row stays one long note from edge to edge, and Spam rows stay straight.

The two ways give slightly different edges even on a plain Fill, since one cuts bent notes and the other fills the bent outline row by row.

The shape's box and its handles stay where they are, and the bent notes may go past the box. A step set to Shape shows ◇ after its name (in Stir's tabs and in Properties). Settings that only choose colours (Squeeze, Colour sets, flips, Colour edges, Folds) are greyed for Shape.

## At the edges (Stir tabs set to Shape)

In a Stir tab set to Shape, At the edges picks what happens to whatever the tab's bend pushes past the shape's box:

• Escape (the start): it stays where the bend put it, past the box.
• Cut off: notes past the box are cut off exactly at its sides.
• Fit inside: the bent shape is shrunk back until it fits in the box. Shrink picks how: Keep proportions (the same amount both ways, so it keeps its look) or Each way on its own (across and up shrink separately). A bend that already stays inside is left as it is.
• Bounce back: what goes past a side folds back in like a mirror. "Edge twice" repeats the edge row once, like the colours' Bounce back, edge twice.
• Wrap around: what goes past a side comes back in from the other side.

Each tab does this right after its own bend, so with two tabs the second one bends what the first left, and can go past the box again unless it has its own choice. A Bounce or Wrap with more steps after it always cuts the notes where bent (an outline can't jump to the other side).

A Liquify set to Shape can also reach past the box: brush outside it and the shape is pushed out there too.

## The order

Shape steps always run first, before any colour step, in this order: the Stir tabs set to Shape (left to right), then the Warp, then the Liquify. After that come all the colour steps (Recolour, Stir tabs set to Colours, Warp, Liquify and the rest) in their usual order. So a colour step always sees the shape already bent. Properties lists the Shape steps at the top and won't let you drag a colour step above them.

"Colours bend too" (beside Moves, ticked at first) keeps the shape's own colours on the notes they were on, so a Colours pattern bends with the shape. Coloured areas bend with the lines either way. Unticked, the colours are laid out again on the bent shape as if it had been drawn that way. It only matters when "Cut notes where bent" is unticked: cut notes always keep their own colours, so it's greyed then.

## Notes cut into pieces

Pasted notes, plain notes, and drawn shapes with "Cut notes where bent" ticked bend note by note. Each note bends like a tiny box: where the bend moves part of a long note to another key it is cut into pieces, so a long note under a twist turns into a slanted run of short notes, while notes that stay on their key stay whole (only stretched or squeezed).

Notes that touched still touch, and each piece keeps its note's velocity, colour and track. Pieces shorter than one tick are dropped. A very strong Twist can cut a shape into millions of pieces; they're made in the background, so the window stays free while they catch up. Notes bent past the top or bottom key or before beat 0 follow "Keep notes made there" like any others ([page:offrange|Notes past the keys]).

For pasted notes this is the only way, so the tick is greyed for them.

Note tool pages, Glue and Sustain work on the bent notes, since the bend comes first.

## Holes and folds

A strong bend can make a line cross another one. Where the shape then overlaps itself, "Overlaps cancel out" leaves a hole there, as with any shape drawn that way. Some Stir bends fold the shape over itself (Straight to round, Kaleidoscope, a very strong twist); then each spot takes the note found where the bend brings it from, the way the colours do. Liquify never folds: its brush just moves the outline.

Where a push or twirl squeezes notes together they bunch up, and where it pulls them apart they spread out: they follow the bend exactly, like a rubber sheet.

## How exact a bend can be

A MIDI note sits on one key and starts and ends on whole ticks, so no bend can be perfectly smooth:
• Up and down, notes move by whole keys. A gentle slope turns into steps, and a very small bend may not move a note at all.
• Along the time, notes start and end on whole ticks. At a low PPQ the bent edges come out coarser; a higher PPQ (under Project) follows the bend more closely.
• Each note has one colour, so a colour bend is only as fine as the notes under it: thin stripes on long notes can look blocky.

Zoomed far in, a smooth curve shows these steps. That's the MIDI format, not a mistake: the notes are as close to the bend as whole keys and ticks allow.

## Good to know

With "Cut notes where bent" unticked only the outline matters: bending the inside of a filled shape changes nothing, and the window says so in orange. Pictures and Hz bass shapes can only bend their colours, so Shape is greyed for them.

The bends are never baked in: come back any time, change a knob, a point or a brush stroke, and the notes are made again. On very big pastes (a million notes) each change's notes take about a second to arrive.
