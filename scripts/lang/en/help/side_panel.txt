## Wider or narrower

Drag the line between the piano roll and the side panel to make the panel wider or narrower. While you drag, a thin line shows where it will go; the panel takes the new width when you let go.

Double-click the line for the smallest width where nothing shown in the panel is cut. Hint lines don't count: they wrap onto more lines instead.

On a wider panel the lists, the Output file box and the Shape box get wider, and hint lines use the room. Buttons, number boxes and short dropdowns keep their size.

Until you drag it, the panel gets wider by itself when something in it would be cut (other Windows versions, display scaling and fonts can make texts wider).

## Shutting it

Drag the line far to the right and the panel shuts into a thin strip at the window's right edge, with a ◀ on it. Click the strip to open the panel again at the width it had, or drag the strip out to the width you want.

View → Side panel shuts and opens it too. The width, and whether the panel is open, are remembered for the next start.

## The drawer

The drawer's side panel can be made wider or narrower the same way, and double-clicked to fit. It doesn't shut.
