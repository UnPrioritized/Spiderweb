Space (or Play) plays from the play line through the MIDI out picked in Settings (Help menu) → Sound; the blue line follows and the view jumps along. Click the bar numbers (or click empty space with Select) to move the play line; dragging along them selects (see Selecting). While playing, clicks don't move the play line (stop first); dragging a box and drawing work as usual, and a right-drag listens on top of what's playing. You can scroll freely while playing: the view waits until you stop for half a second, then jumps back to the play line. Playing stops a quarter bar after the last note.

Built-in BASSMIDI (in the MIDI out list) plays through Spiderweb's own synth, with soundfonts you pick (the Hz bass preview uses the same ones). Pressing Play with no soundfont yet asks for one. Settings (Help menu) → Sound has the Soundfonts list and the Voice limit: how many sounds can ring at once.

Soundfonts can be stacked, and order matters: each soundfont overrides the ones above it. For example, a General MIDI soundfont first and your piano under it: your piano plays the piano, and the General MIDI one adds drums and other instruments. A sound no soundfont has stays silent, like in other players. Add… adds soundfonts at the bottom, Up and Down change the order, Remove takes one out.

When the computer can't keep up, a red "Overloaded" shows at the right end of the status line at the bottom: lower the Voice limit, or tick "Lower it by itself when overloaded" in Settings (Help menu) → Sound. While it lowers the voice limit, the red text says the voices it lets sound now ("Overloaded: 850 voices"); with a loaded MIDI, the notes left out a second and their share ("Overloaded: 12,000 notes left out a second (24 %)").

Or tick "Slow down to keep up" there (off at first): the song then plays slower instead while the computer can't keep up (down to 10 %, the pitch the same), and goes back to its own speed once it can. An orange "Slowed to 62 %" shows while it's slower. With "Lower it by itself when overloaded" ticked too, the voice limit is lowered only once it's at the slowest, or once playing slower stops helping (very many notes ringing at once). Busy loaded MIDIs can slow down a lot.

Right-drag (any tool) = listen to the notes under the mouse, like scrubbing: a second line follows the mouse, the play line stays where it is.

OmniMIDI (KDMAPI) shows in the MIDI out list when OmniMIDI is installed. It plays straight through OmniMIDI with your own OmniMIDI settings, skipping Windows' MIDI layers, so many notes play more smoothly.

Kestrel (GPU, Experimental) in the MIDI out list makes the sound on your graphics card with the same soundfonts, so very busy black MIDIs keep far more notes ringing. See Kestrel (GPU) playback.
