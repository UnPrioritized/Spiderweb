Space (or Play) plays from the play line through the MIDI out picked in Settings (Help menu) → Sound; the blue line follows and the view jumps along. Click the bar numbers (or click empty space with Select) to move the play line; dragging along them selects (see Selecting). Playing stops a quarter bar after the last note.

Built-in BASSMIDI (in the MIDI out list) plays through Spiderweb's own synth, with a soundfont you pick (the Hz bass preview uses the same one). Pressing Play with no soundfont yet asks for one. Settings (Help menu) → Sound changes the soundfont and the Voice limit: how many sounds can ring at once.

When the computer can't keep up, a red "Overloaded" shows at the right end of the status line at the bottom: lower the Voice limit, or tick "Lower it by itself when overloaded" in Settings (Help menu) → Sound.

Right-drag (any tool) = listen to the notes under the mouse, like scrubbing: a second line follows the mouse, the play line stays where it is.

OmniMIDI (KDMAPI) shows in the MIDI out list when OmniMIDI is installed. It plays straight through OmniMIDI with your own OmniMIDI settings, skipping Windows' MIDI layers, so many notes play more smoothly.
