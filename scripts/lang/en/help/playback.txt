Space (or Play) plays from the play line through the MIDI out picked under Project; the blue line follows and the view jumps along. Click or drag the bar numbers (or click empty space with Select) to move the play line. Playing stops a moment after the last note.

Built-in BASSMIDI (in the MIDI out list) plays through Spiderweb's own synth, with a soundfont you pick (the Hz bass preview uses the same one). Pressing Play with no soundfont yet asks for one. Sound settings… (under MIDI out) changes the soundfont and the Voice limit: how many sounds can ring at once.

When the computer can't keep up, a red "Overloaded" shows next to Sound settings…: lower the Voice limit, or tick "Lower it by itself when overloaded" there.

Right-drag (any tool) = listen to the notes under the mouse, like scrubbing.

OmniMIDI (KDMAPI) shows in the MIDI out list when OmniMIDI is installed. It plays straight through OmniMIDI with your own OmniMIDI settings, skipping Windows' MIDI layers, so many notes play more smoothly.
