Ripples is an Inside fill for custom shapes. Rings grow out from the shapes you draw inside the outline, each ring a bigger copy of the shape it grows from. Pick it under Inside in the side panel, like Fill, Spam, Nested and Tiles.

Each ring gets a colour of its own, so set Channels to Multi channel to see them.

## How to draw it

In the custom shape drawer, draw one closed outline, then draw closed shapes inside it (a heart, a star, a circle…). The outer outline is the frame: it makes no rings, it only cuts them off. The shapes inside it are the sources the rings grow from.
[clip:ripples-basic]

Where the rings of two sources meet, the smaller ring wins, so each source takes the space nearest to it.

## Settings

Notes: Fill makes one long note per key in each ring. Spam chops the whole inside into notes of the spam gate, and each note takes the colour of the ring its middle is in. The Normal / Outline dropdown works like Fill's and Spam's.

Steps: how many rings, counting the ones inside the drawn sources.

Colours: how many channels the rings take turns over. The ring just inside each drawn source is always the first colour.

Spacing: 0 = even rings. Above 0 the small rings are closer together, below 0 the big rings are closer together.

Turn: each ring is turned a little more than the one inside it, for a spiral. The ring on each drawn source stays as you drew it, and the rings inside it turn the other way.

Every other one empty: every other ring has no notes, so the rings show as separate bands.

Drawn shapes are ring edges: a ring always ends exactly on each source you drew, so its outline shows in the pattern. Untick it and the rings are spread evenly and may run across it.

Inside them: what goes inside the sources. Rings carry on inside them, Empty leaves them without notes, Plain fills them with one colour.

Overlaps cancel out: on, sources that cross each other cut holes where they overlap, as in any custom shape. Off, every closed-in part is filled.

## Growing past the outline

Grow to: how big the biggest ring is, compared with the source as drawn. With Auto ticked the biggest ring grows just far enough to fill the whole frame.

Cut at the outline: the rings stop at the frame. Untick it to let them grow past it, as far as Grow to says. You can cut them by hand afterwards with the Slice tool.

No frame: when no closed shape holds the others, every shape you drew is a source, and the rings grow out freely to Grow to.

## Centre points

Each source has its own centre point, the small ring with a cross on the selected shape. Its rings grow from there. Drag it with any tool to move it.

Click a centre point to pick that source. The panel then shows Picked source, with its own Turn (Same as all, Other way, or Own with its own angle) and its own Centre, typed as u and v (0 % to 100 % across the source's box).

Right-click a centre point for Back to the middle, Turn the other way and Turn like the others.
