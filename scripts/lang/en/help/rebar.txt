Changes the bar lines without changing the sound: draw a Select box in the [page:conductor|Conductor pane] over the bars, right-click inside it → Change visual time signature…, and type the new one (6/4, 7/8…). The box takes the whole bars it touches, and each of them becomes one bar of the new signature. The notes stretch with the bars and the tempo changes by the same amount, so every note still plays at the same moment: 4/4 at 100 BPM turned into 6/4 becomes 150 BPM. After those bars, the old time signature and tempo come back, and everything later moves along. The box shows which bars it took and what the tempo becomes; with different signatures inside, each bar keeps its own length.

Shapes inside the bars stretch with them (their gate, note tool pages, Sustain, Gate extend / subtract and Hz bass too, so a Hz bass stays in tune). A shape sticking out of the bars is cut at their edge into plain notes, each note moved exactly; Domino pastes stay one piece. Glissando runs and Hz basses with Fixed gates become plain notes too, as does anything that can't keep its sound stretched. The box counts these shapes as you type; when you press OK it first tries the change, and if more shapes would become plain notes it says so and OK becomes Change anyway. One Ctrl+Z takes the whole change back.

It isn't possible over a catch-up or a Keep timing part that reaches over the box's edge (move the box or that part first), over a mask sticking out of the bars, or with a loaded MIDI that has notes after the box's start. Where a note has to land between two ticks it goes to the nearest one: the pane marks those places on the changed bars ("Notes moved by up to a tick", "Notes now under 1 tick apart", "CC moved by up to a tick") until your next change.
