31-EDO splits the octave into 31 equal steps instead of 12. Spiderweb can show and play it when Keys (under Project) is set to 256: then the 31-EDO ticks show next to Keys and under it.

31-EDO: the piano roll shows 31 keys an octave (key 0 = C-1). On the keyboard, white keys are the 7 plain notes, black keys sharps and flats, and the short grey keys half sharps (^) and half flats (v); their rows are white, dark and light. Zoomed in, every row is named.

Audio (only while 31-EDO is ticked): playback and listening with the right mouse button play the notes in 31-EDO, every key up to 255. It works with Built-in BASSMIDI, Kestrel (GPU) or Rendered ahead as the MIDI out; with another one the tick turns orange and playback plays the usual way (keys above 127 skipped).

The pitches follow the 31-EDO template other 31-EDO players use (the 256-key Domino's 31-EDO player, Kestrel), so a saved MIDI sounds the same there. The channels go in groups of three: 1-3, 4-6, 7-9, 11-13 and 14-16. On a group's first channel a row plays as named; on its second channel 2 octaves higher, on its third 4 octaves higher, so the high notes are reached. Channel 10 plays drums, its keys as written; tick "31-EDO: play channel 10 as piano" in Settings → Sound to play it as tuned rows instead, like the other channels.

31-EDO: overlaps keep their pitch (under Multi channel, on by default): Multi channel moves overlapping shapes onto other channels, and in 31-EDO the 2nd and 3rd channel of a group play in other octaves. Ticked, the shapes only go on the first channel of each group (1, 4, 7, 11 and 14), then on the same five channels of the next port, so overlaps keep their pitch in Spiderweb and in the saved MIDI. Unticked, Multi channel uses channels 1-16 as usual.

Play the loaded MIDI in 31-EDO (under Keys; with Audio ticked and a MIDI loaded): a loaded MIDI plays as written at first, so its notes don't follow the 31-EDO rows. Ticked, its notes play tuned like yours, every key up to 255, each on its own channel's octaves (its channel 10 as drums, unless set to piano). The tuning needs every channel's pitch bend for itself, so it all plays as one piano sound: the MIDI's pitch bends, instrument changes, volume / expression, sustain pedal and other controls are left out (the status line counts them at Play).

Only the look and the sound here change: the saved MIDI and Copy to Domino keep the same key numbers, so play it in a player set up for 31-EDO. The ticks are saved with the project; the saved MIDI and Copy to Domino are the same with "Play the loaded MIDI in 31-EDO" ticked.
