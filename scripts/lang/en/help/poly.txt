Click point after point, or drag each piece. Double-click, right-click or Enter finishes it; Esc cancels.

Selected polyline: drag its squares to move points; middle-click adds a point at the mouse, put in the line where it fits best.

Last note (panel) works at every point: each piece is its own line.
