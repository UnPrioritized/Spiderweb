Tick Velocity pane (top) to show or hide it. The pane under the piano roll shows every note's velocity. Draw over the bars with Linear, Curve, Pencil or [page:formulas|Formula]. With a shape selected, only its notes change (the others fade); with nothing selected, every note under the drag.

Select (the first button): drag across the pane to pick the shapes in that time, like dragging along the bar numbers. Drag inside the box to move them in time (Ctrl = copy, Shift = free); a click outside drops it. Double right-click switches between Select and the tool you used last.

Formula: drag it like Linear; a pattern (Wave, Zigzag, Bounce or one you saved) swings the velocity up and down around the line. Pick the pattern, its Loops and height (in velocity steps) next to the tool buttons: changing them changes the line you just drew, until Enter or a click elsewhere.

Linear / Curve: drag it, then drag its end squares to move the ends (Ctrl = level with the other end) or a curve's round handle to bend it. Ctrl while drawing = flat. Lines are drawn on whole ticks. Moving an end square afterwards snaps it onto a nearby note start (hold Shift for whole ticks only). Enter, Esc or a click outside the pane = done (Esc or Ctrl+Z while the mouse is still held throws the line away).

The velocities move and stretch with the shape. Typing a start / end velocity in the panel goes back to a straight ramp. Drag the bar above the pane to resize it.

Greyed bars behind the others are only shown, never changed by a line: notes kept past the top or bottom key or before the start (see [page:offrange|Notes past the keys]), and, with "Split around instead of stretching" ticked, the part of a note that comes back after a later note on the same key.
