Select one or more shapes, then right-click one: Claw machine… (or press Alt+W). The notes change on the piano roll while you try settings. Accept keeps the change (one Ctrl+Z takes it back); X or Esc puts the notes back as they were. Reset sets the page back to the start, where it changes nothing.
[clip:claw-window]

Pages: each time you open the window it starts on +, a new page. Change a setting and the page goes on top of the shape's other pages, working on the notes they left, so the same tool can be used again and again.

The row at the top has a tab for each page this shape has (numbered by its place among all its Claw machine, Strum, Chop and Copy along a path pages; the tab's tooltip shows the whole order). Click a tab to change that page, untick On to switch it off for now, or click the small × beside its tab to take it off (right-clicking a tab does both too).

You can keep working in the main window while it's open. It follows the selection: select other shapes to work on them. When you select other shapes or change something in the main window, the claw tried so far is kept (as its own Ctrl+Z step).

The pages are kept with the shape: the outline stays as you drew it, and moving, resizing or reshaping it makes new notes that go through the same pages again.

Flipping or turning the shape (Turn 90°, or dragging just outside a custom shape's corner) flips or turns what they made, where it is. Turned, each note becomes short notes on the key rows it crosses (a column of them for 90°), so there are more notes.

Images take no note tools: Claw machine, Strum, Chop, glue and Sustain skip them.

Velocities drawn or typed afterwards replace theirs; a page added after that works on the new velocities.
[clip:claw-time]

Mode By time works like the claw machine found in other piano rolls. The mouse wheel over a dropdown (Mode, Period, Trash every) picks the next or previous choice.

Period: the claw works on this much time at a time, over and over, starting at the shape's first note.

Trash every: each period is cut into equal slices and one is thrown away. 1/4 = the first quarter, 2/4 = the second quarter, and so on. Notes in that slice go, notes crossing its edges are cut there, and all later notes move earlier to close the gap, so the notes end sooner.

Time dist: bends time inside each period. Turned right: long notes first, then faster and faster (a bouncing ball, a drum roll before a drop). Turned left: the other way round. It works even with Trash every at (none).

Turn Time dist by dragging it up / down (Shift = fine), with the mouse wheel or the arrow keys; it stops at 0 for a moment on the way past. Hold the right mouse button on it to point it at the mouse. Middle-click (press the mouse wheel) puts it back to 0.

Stretch to compensate: stretches everything back to the shape's full length, so the notes get longer instead of ending sooner.

Remove short notes: removes the tiny pieces the claw leaves (under 1/24 beat). Notes that were that short already stay.

Count notes / Count keys / Count chords: keep some, then trash some, over and over to the end. Notes are counted in time order; keys are counted up from the shape's lowest key (keys with no notes count too), so every other key makes stripes; a chord = all the notes starting at the same moment.

Random: keeps about that many notes in every hundred. New random picks other notes; the same shape always gets the same pick.

Shorten instead of remove (not in By time): the notes picked to go stay, only shorter: this much of their length.
