Deform… (in the drawer's side panel, under the library) bends the drawing itself: its strokes change shape the way [page:stir|Stir] bends colours on the piano roll. It changes the strokes like editing them by hand: nothing is remembered afterwards, and Ctrl+Z undoes it.

With strokes picked ([page:drawer_select|Select tool]), only those bend; otherwise the whole drawing, except locked layers (hidden ones bend too). With Mirror on, the mirrored copies bend as mirror images, so the drawing stays symmetric.

## The bends

Turn turns the strokes round the Centre. Twist turns them more the farther they are from the Centre, like a whirlpool. Bulge / Pinch swells the middle out or pulls it in. Ripple makes waves: Ripple moves picks whether points move sideways, up and down, or in and out in rings round the Centre; Wave length sets how far apart the waves are.

Copies (its own box under Bends) makes turned copies in one go. At 1, Turn turns the strokes as usual. At 2 or more, the strokes stay where they are and copies are added, each turned by Turn more than the one before: one line with Turn 22.5 and Copies 16 becomes the 16 spokes of a wheel (a line straight across the circle needs only 8, turned 22.5 each). A copy that would lie exactly on a stroke already there is left out, so a circle round the Centre isn't stacked on itself and turning past a full circle adds nothing twice. Hidden strokes bend but aren't copied. The copies come out selected with the strokes. Copies can't be used while Mirror is on.

All the bends work together and show live on the board. Apply keeps them; Cancel, Esc or the window's X puts everything back.

Each slider has a number box beside it for exact numbers, also past the slider's range. Middle-click a slider to put it back to 0. Dragging Turn, Twist or Bulge near 0 stops there. The boxes work like the program's other number boxes: Up / Down, the mouse wheel once clicked, or drag the box or its name sideways (Shift = big steps, Ctrl = fine).

## The Centre and the area

The Centre is the green ring on the board. It starts in the middle of the strokes. Drag it to move it; right-click it to put it back. The Centre, the area and its handles snap to the grid while you drag them (hold Shift to place them freely), and Ctrl+Z works in the drawer too while Deform is open.

Area limits the bends to part of the drawing, like Stir's area: a Circle, Square or Diamond, or any drawing from your library (its inside counts). Its dashed line shows where the bending stops; inside it fades out softly toward the line, or stops sharp with Hard edge. Points outside the area stay exactly where they are. Bulge inside an area swells round the area's own middle.

On the board, drag inside the area to move it. Its green box sizes it: a corner or side handle stretches it, Ctrl on a corner makes it bigger or smaller and keeps its shape. Drag just outside a corner to turn it (15° steps, Shift = free), or just outside a side's middle to slant it. A small area has no box: drag its line to size it. Size, Position, Stretch, Angle and Slant can also be typed in the window. Position is where the area's middle is, in % of the board: 50 across and 50 up puts it in the board's middle.

Cut off outside the area cuts away whatever the bend pushes past the area's line, so a turned line stays inside it. Put the area over your outline (a Square over a drawn square, for example), tick Hard edge for the full bend right up to the line, then turn. Only the strokes Deform bends are cut (copies too); the others stay whole, even past the line. Cut pieces become plain lines, a stroke wholly outside goes, and the pieces come out selected. It can't be used while Mirror is on.

## What happens to the strokes

Strokes get extra points only where the bend curves them; a straight side the bend doesn't touch stays two points. Curves stay curves with handles you can still edit. Circles, arcs, freehand strokes, polygon sides and strokes with a [page:formulas|Formula] become fine polylines. A curve bent very hard (short ripples) also becomes a fine line; the window says how many before you Apply.

Points stuck on other lines stay on them, so closed areas stay closed, and coloured areas follow their strokes. While a slider moves, the colours wait and catch up a moment after you stop.

While the window is open, the rest of the drawer waits; the wheel and middle-drag still move the view.
