The red line is the tone you hear; the middle of a row is the exact tone (a green line when you zoom in). It bends with everything that moves the tone: slides, Glide, note bends, the Pitch line and envelope, and the Vibrato's wave (fading in with its line). Untick Red line (at the top of the window) to hide the line and its dots; slides and tunes stay.

Every repeat lasts a whole number of ticks, so each one is a little off, more at a low PPQ: the faint line behind the red one shows every repeat's own pitch.

With Alternating gates the repeats take turns being too high and too low (the faint line jumps) and the average, the red line, is exact. With Fixed gates every repeat is off the same way, so the red line itself sits off to one side.

Zoomed in, drag the red line in a note up or down to tune that note alone (whole cents, Shift = finer; it sticks to the exact tone). Double-click the red line to put the tune back to 0. Right-click a note and pick Tune… to type it.

In the window's Hz settings…: Pitch moves every tone in cents (100 = one key). Gates: Alternating = gates of two sizes take turns so the tone is exact; Fixed = every gate is a whole number of ticks, a little off.

Hz shape… (in Hz settings…) sets how the notes look inside each wave and how long they last: see the Hz shape page.

Auto gates (what a new Hz bass starts with): a note that holds still gets Fixed gates when they're off its tone by no more than the threshold (the number next to Gates, in cents), else Alternating ones. A slide is always Alternating.

A note can also have gates of its own: right-click it → Use fixed gates or Use alternating gates. Picking from the Gates list in Hz settings… puts every note back on the Hz bass's gates.

In the window a band around each note's tone shows the threshold: green = Fixed, orange = Alternating. Hold the mouse over a note to see how far off Fixed gates would be. Right-click a note and pick Own Auto threshold… to give it (and the other selected notes) a threshold of its own. About 5 cents is where most ears start to hear it; the highest is 50 (half a key).
