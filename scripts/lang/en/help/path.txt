Select one or more shapes, then right-click one: Copy along a path…. The shape is copied again and again along a path from A to B, and on through more points if you add them. The copies change on the piano roll while you try settings. Accept keeps them (one Ctrl+Z takes them back); X or Esc puts the notes back as they were.

Click on the piano roll where the path ends: that's B. Until you click, the copies follow the mouse. Then drag B, or the round middle point, to bend the path. Going up, A is the shape's bottom-left corner and B the last copy's top-right corner; going down, the corners swap, so the copies always stay between the points.

Add point puts another point after the last one: it follows the mouse until you click. Right-click a point to remove it. The boxes under the settings show the picked point as bar, beat, tick and key: type there to put it exactly. Shift while dragging = off the grid.

Like the Claw machine, the path is a page kept with the shape: moving or reshaping the shape moves the path and every copy along with it. Several selected shapes travel together as one group. Opening it from the menu on a shape that has a path shows its last path; click + on top for another one, a tab to change one the shape has, or the small × beside a tab to take it off.

Path: Straight, Curve (a bend through the round middle point) or Arc (a piece of a circle through it). Dragging a middle point on a straight path makes it a curve.

Formula: a pattern laid along the whole path, like a curve's Formula (Wave, Zigzag, Curls…, or your own saved ones). Loops = how many times it repeats from A to the last point; the other boxes are the numbers in its formula, like height. Custom… opens the formula window to pick, change, draw or type one.

Spacing: One copy every (a note length, or ticks) puts a copy each time the path goes that much later; Number of copies spreads that many evenly along it (the shape itself is the first). Copies on the points: a copy's corner sits right on each point. Half copies at the points: where the path turns, the copies carry on past the point, and only the parts on the near side are kept, so the turn is as full as the rest.

Speed: Even, Speed up (close together at A, further apart towards the end) or Slow down.

Size: how big the first and the last copy are, in %; the ones between grow or shrink step by step.

Colours: Keep, Shift all channels, Turn within own colours, or One per copy, each Step channels on from the one before. Needs Multi channel.

Flip: Each copy going down flips the copies where the path goes down (upside down, left-right or both). Mirror at the peak: past the path's first peak or dip, the copies are the ones before it mirrored.

On top: where copies cover each other, which one is drawn on top. The one under it is cut away there, note by note.

Range: Keep inside keeps only the notes from the lowest key to the highest key, the shape's own too; the rest are cut off. These keys stay put when the shape moves (dotted lines on the piano roll).

Turn into plain shapes: every copy becomes a shape of its own notes, in the colours it showed, so you can move, cut or change each one by hand. With Mirror at the peak, a copy and its mirror image are two shapes. The shape itself stays as drawn, without this path. The window closes; one Ctrl+Z takes it back.

Notes past the lowest or highest key, or before the song's start, are cut off.
