Drag (or click) corner to corner; Ctrl makes it a perfect square. While you drag, its sides stick too: near another stroke's slanted or curved line, the square grows or shrinks until it rests on it (a corner on the line, or a side against a curve). This doesn't happen with Ctrl, and Shift draws freely. Between two lines it can rest on both at once (a smaller reach, with a mark at each touch). The corner under the mouse sticking to a point or a crossing still comes first.
