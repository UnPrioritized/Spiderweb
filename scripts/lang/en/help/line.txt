Drag from the start to the end, or click the start and click the end (the line follows the mouse in between; right-click or Esc cancels).

Each key the line crosses gets one note, starting where the line reaches that key, so a slope becomes a smooth staircase. Points snap to the grid (Snap at the top); hold Shift to place them freely.

Last note (panel): the last note ends on the line's end, or starts exactly on it.

Right-click it: Formula (a shape or a pattern along it, see Formulas), Tumours… (bumps along it, see Tumours), Copy along a path… (see Copy along a path) and Split here (see Join). With two open lines selected: Add between… (see Add between).
