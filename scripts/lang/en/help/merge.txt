Select two shapes, right-click one of them → Gate sensitive merge, then pick a side. Merge to right: the shape on the left slides right. Merge to left: the shape on the right slides left. Where you right-click doesn't matter.

Each key row of the sliding shape moves on its own, until its note meets the other shape's note on the same key (it ends where that one starts). The notes keep their lengths, so a straight block takes on the other shape's slanted edge.

Both become one shape ("Merged notes" in the Shapes list), and it's selected. Rows that have nothing to meet slide by the smallest slide and become a shape of their own, "Merge leftovers". If no row meets anything, nothing is merged.

A merged shape can be moved, turned, slanted and stretched like a custom shape, and the custom panel's settings go into both parts. Each part keeps its own colours. When both shapes are sustained with the same settings, the merged shape is sustained too. Split into separate shapes (Ctrl+Shift+G) gives the two parts back.

Images can't be merged.
