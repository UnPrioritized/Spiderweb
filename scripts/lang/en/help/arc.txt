A piece of a perfect circle. Click its start, a point it passes through, then its end. Or drag from the start to the end, then move the mouse to bend it and click.

It's round as the piano roll looks while you draw it. Put the end on the start for a whole circle.

Selected: drag its round middle point (any tool) to bend it, its squares to move the ends. Right-click it: Formula (a shape or a pattern along it, see Formulas).
