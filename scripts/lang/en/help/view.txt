Wheel = scroll up / down, Shift+wheel = sideways. Ctrl+Alt+wheel = zoom both ways, Ctrl+Shift+wheel = zoom time, Alt+wheel = zoom pitch. Ctrl+wheel = the next / previous track (shown alone, its name on the status line; down goes to the next one). Middle-drag scrolls. Fit view shows everything. The zoom and scroll are remembered.

Scrollbars (under and right of the piano roll): drag the bar to scroll, drag one of its two ends to zoom, click beside it to go one screen that way. The − and + buttons at the end of each scrollbar zoom out / in. The bar under the piano roll reaches 8 bars past the last note.

Show lines / Show notes (top) hide the drawn lines or the notes. Keys outside a real 88-key piano are greyed on the keyboard (with 256 keys: the keys above the usual 128). A shape wholly above the top key shows a small red up arrow at the top of the piano roll: click it to grab the shape.

Space past the edges (Settings → Piano roll): "Space past the keys" lets the piano roll go 1 to 4 keyboard heights below the lowest key and above the highest one, and "Space before the start" up to 16 bars before the first beat. The extra space is drawn darker, with the key rows and bar lines going on. Scroll there, move shapes, points and handles there, draw there, or start a Select box there; no notes are made there (unless Keep notes made there is ticked: then they're kept see-through, see Notes past the keys). A shape partly out there makes its notes as a whole (velocity, note tool pages, Sustain…) and only the part inside the keys, from the first beat on, is kept; a note across the first beat starts on it. Dragging something past the edge of the piano roll scrolls the view along. With every key row shown, the scrollbar beside the keys can still be dragged into the extra space, or pushed on past its end; dragging one of its ends catches on the keyboard's edge. Turn a setting down with shapes still out there and each one shows a small arrow at the top, bottom or left edge: click it to grab the shape.

Snap and grid: Snap (top) is the grid that points, moves and pastes snap to (Shift = free). Off, Bar, or a note length: a note with a dot = dotted (3/8 = a dotted quarter), a 3 = triplets (1/6 = quarter-note triplets). Custom… opens Customised snap: a number of notes (3 / 16 = three 16th notes), a dotted (.) or double-dotted (..) note, divided by a number (3 = triplets); it shows how long that is in beats and ticks.
