Press Tab to swap the piano roll for the track list, and Tab again to go back. It shows the tracks your MIDI will have: one row per track, in the same order as Export MIDI and Export to Domino write them, each with a small picture of its notes in its channel's colour. The Conductor row on top marks every tempo change and time or key signature.

Spiderweb picks the tracks itself (see Channels), so the list only shows them: use it to check which shapes land on which track before you export. Rest the mouse on a track's name to see its channel, how many notes it has and which shapes they come from. Long names end in "…" and show whole there.

Zoom and scroll with the scrollbars, like the piano roll: drag an end of the bar under the list to zoom in time, drag an end of the bar on its right to make the rows taller or shorter. Ctrl+wheel zooms in time, Ctrl+Shift+wheel changes the rows' height, Shift+wheel scrolls sideways. Zoomed far out, each column holds 2, 4, 8... bars, so the bar numbers never run into each other.

Click a track to highlight it. Press Tab and the piano roll shows only that track's notes, its name in the piano roll's top left corner. Every other track is hidden until you click the Conductor row in the track list (nothing highlighted shows every track again). Hidden notes still play and still go into the files.

## Show multiple tracks

Show multiple tracks… picks the tracks the piano roll shows faintly behind the highlighted one. Ctrl+click a row does the same. Those rows get a dot in their colour. Faint sets how strongly they show (0 % hides them).

## Mute

Click a track's speaker to mute it: it isn't played until you click it again, and its picture turns grey. Muting only changes what you hear: Export MIDI and Export to Domino still write the track.

## Snap

The track list has its own snap (Bar at first). It's where a click on the track list's bar numbers puts the play line. Hold Shift for no snap.

Shortcuts like Delete or Ctrl+A do nothing while the track list shows, so nothing changes out of sight. Space still plays.
