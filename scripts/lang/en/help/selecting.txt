With Select, drag on empty space: the box selects every shape it touches (Ctrl+drag adds them). The box snaps to the grid, out to the next grid line as soon as the mouse crosses one (Shift = no snap). Ctrl+click selects more shapes (also in the Shapes list), Ctrl+A selects all.

The box stays after you let go. Drag its sides or corners to stretch the shapes it selected (the other side stays put), or drag inside it to move them. Dragged past the edge of the piano roll, the view scrolls.

Drag along the bar numbers (with any tool) to select every key in that time: the box covers the whole keyboard and the bar numbers turn black above it. Drag the black part sideways to move what it selected in time only; click it to move the play line.

Ctrl+drag after a box draws another box (they all count together). Ctrl+drag inside the boxes moves a copy of everything in them.

Right-click inside the boxes with two or more shapes selected: a menu with what works on all of them at once (Formula, Tumours, note tools, Join, Turn into live shape, Add between, Copy along a path, Mask, flip, turn, delete…).

Click a shape that's already selected to pick the next shape under it (handy where shapes overlap).

Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V: copy / paste at the play line (snapped to the grid); with Select, double-click empty space to paste there. Ctrl+C also puts the shapes on the clipboard as text, to share them (see Sharing shapes). Ctrl+X: cut (copy, then delete). Ctrl+D: duplicate. Del: delete.

Ctrl+H / Ctrl+J: flip sideways / upside down (sideways also mirrors velocities).

Ctrl+Left / Ctrl+Right: turn 90° as it looks on screen (the zoom decides how many beats a key becomes).

Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z): undo / redo. While something is half drawn, Ctrl+Z just drops that.

Settings in the panel (velocity, last note…) change every selected shape. Where the selected shapes differ, a dropdown says Mixed, a number box is empty and a tick box is half-ticked: picking or typing a value sets it on all of them.
