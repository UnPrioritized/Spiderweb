Ctrl+Z undoes the last change, Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) does it again. Ctrl+Z while something is half drawn drops just that.

Every change is a step with a name (the last 300 are kept). Tick History in the toolbar to see them all and click any step to jump straight to it (see History).

The drawer has its own Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y for its drawing.
