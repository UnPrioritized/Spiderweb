Pick Slice (K) and drag a straight line across shapes. When you let go, every line, polyline, freehand stroke, curve and arc it crosses is cut in two there (more pieces if it crosses more than once). The pieces stay where they were and end up selected.
[clip:slice-line]

Custom shapes (filled ones too) are cut in two when the line goes all the way across them: each half keeps its side of the drawing and gets the cut as a new edge, so a filled shape becomes two filled shapes. Areas coloured by hand stay with the half they're in. A spam gate Range is shared out: each half gets the part of it that was there, so the gates stay where they were. A line that only goes part of the way in leaves the shape whole.

Funnels are cut through their notes: the line has to go all the way across them. Each piece keeps the funnel's notes on its side (a note across the line is cut in two there) and shows only its side of the funnel. A setting changed on a piece (gate, Inside, wall) changes only that piece. Changing a piece's points or curves (or Turn into a complete shape) makes it a whole funnel again, with all its notes.

Pasted notes are cut by time, and the line doesn't have to go all the way across: on each key row it crosses, the notes before the line become a new shape and a note it goes through is cut in two there. Rows it doesn't cross stay whole, together with the notes after the line. Both pieces are ordinary pasted notes. A velocity line, glue or pages on the pasted notes are kept in the pieces' notes as they sounded, but can't be changed any more.

Only some shapes or part of them: draw a Select box (or several, with Ctrl) first, then pick Slice. Only the shapes the boxes selected are cut, and only where the slice line is inside a box.

The line's ends go to the snap grid in time and between two keys, so a flat cut runs between key rows. Hold Shift to place them exactly. Text, pictures, merged shapes (Gate sensitive merge) and Hz bass aren't cut.

A shape with glue or Claw machine, Strum or Chop pages is cut through its notes instead, wherever they are (they can sit far from the drawing): the line has to go all the way across them. Both pieces keep the whole drawing, one on top of the other until you move one. The pages are done before the cut, so the pieces look exactly as the shape did. Pages added to a piece afterwards work on that piece only. Changing a piece's outline (or Turn into a complete shape) makes it a shape of its own: it gets all of the shape's notes back and the pages work on them again, so its look may change.

To freeze a shape, right-click it → Turn into plain notes. It becomes pasted notes, exactly as it sounds now, and each note keeps its channel. Then Slice cuts it by time like any pasted notes. It works on merged shapes too.

One Ctrl+Z puts everything back as it was.
