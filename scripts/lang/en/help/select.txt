Click a shape (its line or one of its notes) to select it (where shapes overlap: a line first, then the one on top; a selected shape under the mouse stays the one you drag); Ctrl+click selects more (Ctrl+A = all). Drag a shape to move it (all selected ones move together), drag its squares to reshape it.

Drag on empty space to draw a box: it selects every shape it touches (its line or one of its notes). Ctrl+drag adds them to the ones selected. Click empty space (no drag) to move the play line there.

Right-click a shape (or one of its notes) = its menu (delete, duplicate, copy, flip, turn, add a point…). Right-click empty space = deselect. Double right-click switches between Select and the last drawing tool.

Blue curve handles can be dragged with any tool.
