In the Hz bass window, pressing the mouse places a note at once, and it follows the mouse until you let go. A note that is there moves the same way; drag either end to change its length. A new note is as long as the last one you clicked or resized. While you hold a note, its key sounds on the MIDI-out device.

Select (V) and Pencil (P) are at the top of the window; double right-click switches between the two. With Select, dragging on empty space draws a box that selects the notes it touches (Ctrl adds them); with Pencil, Ctrl+drag does it. The box snaps to the grid like on the piano roll.

Drag the box's right side to make all its notes longer or shorter by the same amount (they keep their starts), or drag inside it to move them.

Ctrl+click picks several, Ctrl+A all; Delete removes the selected notes. Double-click a note to delete it.

Drawing tools (the button after Bend, ▾ for the list): Line (L), Polyline (Y), Freehand (F), Curve (C) and Arc (A) draw a path that becomes notes joined by slides, so the tone follows it. Faint notes show what it will make, and its points can still be dragged.

Enter, right-click or another tool makes the notes; Esc throws the path away. Points snap to the grid and to whole keys (Shift = free).

Ctrl+C copies the selected notes, Ctrl+X cuts them (copies, then deletes) and Ctrl+V pastes them at the play line (the preview's, or the main window's when the preview is off); with Select, double-click empty space to paste them there. With the preview on, a click on empty space with Select moves the play line there.

Notes copied in Domino after that paste the same way (Windows only): each copied track goes into its own layer, the first into the picked layer and the next ones into the layers under it (new layers are added as needed). A note's velocity becomes its own loudness line. Ticks are taken as they are: use the same PPQ.

The window has its own Snap (Shift = no snap). The mouse wheel scrolls, Shift+wheel goes sideways, Ctrl+wheel zooms, the middle button drags the view.
