Right-click a curve → Symmetric halves: one half follows the other. The curve gets an odd number of anchors with the middle one on the line of symmetry; editing one half rewrites the other.

Mirrored (like an arch): the second half is the first one reflected.
[clip:symmetric-arch]

Turned half way round (like an S): the second half is the first one turned around the middle.
[clip:symmetric-s]

Off: the halves go their own way again.
