A selected custom shape (and text) shows a box. The mouse pointer changes to show what a drag will do.

Move: drag anywhere inside it (Select tool). Its earliest note snaps to the grid, not the box; Shift = free.
[clip:custom_edit-move]

Resize: drag a corner square. The opposite corner stays put; Ctrl keeps its proportions. The notes on the side you drag snap to the grid (Shift = free).
[clip:custom_edit-resize]

One side: drag a side (or its middle square) to move just that side.
[clip:custom_edit-sides]

Turn: drag from just outside a corner. It turns around its middle in 15° steps; Shift = any angle.
[clip:custom_edit-turn]

Skew: drag from just outside a side's middle. That side slides along itself in grid steps (Shift = free) while the opposite side stays.
[clip:custom_edit-skew]

Turning happens as the piano roll looks; zooming only one way afterwards slants it.

After a turn or skew, right-click the shape → Fit box to drawing: the box is straight again and hugs the drawing, which stays where it is.
