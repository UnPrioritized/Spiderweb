A selected custom shape (and text) shows a box. The mouse pointer changes to show what a drag will do.

Move: drag anywhere inside it ([page:select|Select tool]). Its notes snap to the grid, not the box: the start of its earliest note or the end of its last note, whichever is nearer the mouse; Shift = free. Shapes moved with a Select box follow the box instead (see [page:selecting|Selecting]).
[clip:custom_edit-move]

Resize: drag a corner square. The opposite corner stays put; Ctrl keeps its proportions. The notes on the side you drag snap to the grid (Shift = free). Its top and bottom stay on the lines between keys, covering the key under the mouse; dragging to the bottom and top keys covers every key. A shape whose top or bottom sits on the middle of a key (from an older version, set in Properties, or moved with Shift): only the side you drag goes onto a line; the other side stays where it is until you drag it too.
[clip:custom_edit-resize]

One side: drag a side (or its middle square) to move just that side.
[clip:custom_edit-sides]

Turn: drag from just outside a corner. It turns around its middle in 15° steps; Shift = any angle.
[clip:custom_edit-turn]

For an exact angle, right-click → Turn → Turn by… and type it (80, 22.5, decimals too): plus turns right (clockwise), minus turns left. The shape turns as you type; OK keeps it, Esc puts it back. Several selected shapes turn together around their middle. It works for shapes with a box (drawn shapes, text, pasted notes, pictures); lines turn only 90° at a time.

Skew: drag from just outside a side's middle. That side slides along itself in grid steps (Shift = free) while the opposite side stays.
[clip:custom_edit-skew]

Turning happens as the piano roll looks; zooming only one way afterwards slants it.

After a turn or skew, right-click the shape → Fit box to drawing: the box is straight again and hugs the drawing, which stays where it is.
