What changed in each version, newest first. This page opens by itself the first time a new version starts.

## Version 1.6.2

What's new:

• Lasso tool (J): draw a loop round notes and press Enter to cut out what's inside, a bite or a hole, and select it. Drag round, click points (Polygonal) or let it cling to the notes without cutting any (Magnetic). The loop waits for Enter, so you can drag its points and bend it with curve handles first. The drawer has it too, for cutting strokes. See Help → Lasso.

• Conductor pane (View → Conductor pane): draw the tempo as a line under the piano roll, with jumps, glides, curves and formulas, and copy and paste it. Catch-up runs fast after a stop until the music is back on time. Keep timing changes the tempo while the music sounds the same, and Fit bar lines keeps the bar lines on the bars you hear. The ruler shows every tempo change. See Help → Conductor pane.

• Time and key signatures: right-click in the Conductor pane. Bar lines and numbers follow them everywhere. They replace the Beats per bar box, and the start tempo is now the Conductor pane's point at bar 1 (no BPM box; old projects get that point by themselves).

• A Hz bass stays in tune where the tempo changes under it. When the tempo there changes later, it says so in orange, with an Update button.

• Paste from Domino brings the Conductor track's tempo and signatures, and keeps the notes' CC, pitch bend and program changes: Copy / Export to Domino give them back exactly. Copy to Domino carries the tempo and signatures too.

• Menu bar: File, Edit, Shape, Tools, View and Help. File → New starts a new MIDI, Save writes into the file you opened, and the title bar shows the file's name (with * while there are unsaved changes).

• A pasted shape as big as a whole song no longer covers the piano roll: click between its notes to drop the selection or place the play line. Esc drops the selection.

• Hz bass MIDI CC lines: Cutoff, Expression, Channel volume, Pan and Pitch wheel, under a "MIDI CC" row in the Effects pane. They make no notes: they go into the MIDI as messages, and the player changes the sound with them. The synth window's Filter box (MIDI CC) draws the Cutoff line like a synth's filter, and the MOD tab can move its knobs. Voice box → CC → Own channel per note gives every note its own channel and its own lines (an Arpeggio's steps play on the held note's channel). The live keys play them too. The Hz bass window says in orange when the lines also change other shapes. See Help → Hz bass MIDI CC lines.

• The mouse wheel changes a number box, dropdown or knob only after you click it, everywhere in Spiderweb. Scrolling a panel or the synth window past them no longer changes them by accident.

• Gate subtract (right-click → Gate subtract…): every note gets a little shorter, so a blank space shows between notes that touched. Take it from the note end, the note start or both. The amount can change across the shape along a graph, by time, by key or along each key row. It comes last, so Strum, Claw and the other note tools work as before. Flipping sideways swaps the end and the start, so a flipped copy mirrors its gaps.

• Gate sensitive merge has its own window: Up, Left, Right or Down, and Keep shapes separate to merge any number of shapes, each left as it is, with the moving one turned into fixed notes. Merging up or down works as one shape too. The window says which shape moves, or why it can't merge.

• Ruler selecting: drag along the bar numbers (with any tool) to select every key in that time. Drag the black bar to move it in time, Ctrl+drag for a copy, right-click it for the selection's menu.

• Select boxes stay while you draw with another tool, and a shape drawn inside one joins the selection. Right-clicking empty space no longer drops it.

• Moving a shape snaps the end of its last note to the grid too: the start or the end, whichever is nearer the mouse.

• Moving with a Select box snaps the box's left side to the grid, and notes off the grid stay off it by the same amount, like in many MIDI programs. Everything in the box stops together at beat 0. The Hz bass window works the same way.

• Hz bass CC lines run on a little after the sound ends, then go back to where players start. Set how long in the Hz bass window's Settings… → CC run-on (Auto measures it from the soundfont).

• Hz bass window: drag along the bar numbers to select every key, Select boxes stay while you draw, and a box over nothing can be moved and stretched, like on the piano roll.

• Right-drag listening shows its own line and leaves the play line where it was.

• Plain notes (Turn into plain notes, path copies turned into plain shapes) follow Settings → General → Overlapping shapes keep their colours: off, an overlapping one takes the next free channels.

• Fixes: merging no longer leaves gaps where a row is shorter; plain shapes keep their colours apart and no longer move a channel onto channel 10; quick right-clicks no longer switch tools; no more typing lag in the output name, BPM and beats per bar boxes; texts no longer get cut off with wider system fonts; the toolbar's pinned tools wrap and Help stays at the top right; Delete and Ctrl+A work after clicking a toolbar button, also in the Hz bass window.

## Version 1.6.1

What's new:

• Inside fill Ripples: rings grow out from the shapes you draw inside a custom shape's outline, each ring a bigger copy of its shape and a colour of its own. Where the rings of two shapes meet, the smaller one wins. Steps, Colours, Spacing, Turn for spirals, and Grow to. Without an outer outline, or with Cut at the outline unticked, the rings grow out freely. Each shape's centre point is a handle: drag it, or click it to give that shape its own turn and centre.

• Settings → General → Overlapping shapes keep their colours: with Multi channel, a shape that overlaps another moves whole to the next row of channels (15 further, 16 with channel 10), so it keeps its colours.

• Slice tool: a flat line through pasted notes now cuts them into the notes above it and the rest. Only the notes within the line's length are lifted out.

• Inside fills are now a dropdown, with a line before the patterns; fills that close gaps show in orange. Nested, Tiles and Ripples keep their settings when you pick another fill and come back.

• Right-click menu: Turn into plain notes (the shape's notes become pasted notes you can Slice) and Fit box to drawing (the box hugs the drawing, the notes don't change).

• Text: Direction (Piano roll, Player across, Player down) for falling-notes players, plus Letter turn and Flip letters. Right-click a text for Split into letters or Turn into custom shape. A new text's first note starts at the click.

• Multi channel: New channels each Colours round (with Fill each round with empty tracks), and Channels 1-16 in order under Use channel 10. Merged shapes whose parts have their own Colours get Colours rounds too.

• Settings → General → (Experimental) Domino 256 keys: for the 256 keys mod of Domino. Copy, Export and Paste keep keys above 127.

• Copy and Export to Domino are much faster with many notes or tracks (they use several cores) and no longer ask past 4,096 tracks.

• Drawer Mirror: mirrored strokes stay linked, so dragging, deleting, hiding, flipping, turning or colouring one changes its copies too. Ctrl+Shift drag changes one side only; the stroke menu has Unlink mirror and Mirror this stroke again.

• Moving and resizing snap the notes to the grid, not the box.

• Claw keeps only the longest note when notes land on the same key and tick. Strum goes up to 1000 bars.

• Window: MIDI out is only in Settings → Sound, Settings opens on General, the Help button sits at the top right beside Settings, and the Overloaded warning is at the right end of the status line. The Project box's Channels rows can be folded. Help pages have headings and more pictures, and match the program again.

• Fixes: flipped shapes mirror their notes exactly; outline tips and squares on a key edge no longer add stray notes; sliced pieces keep their notes and area colours when moved; turned or flipped texts no longer jump when retyped; Nested, Tiles and Ripples with Colours use every colour; the toolbar's Play button is no longer cut off in narrow windows; the mouse wheel on dropdowns works right after undo; MIDI export keeps the channel number of empty tracks.

## Version 1.6.0

What's new:

• Inside fill Nested: smaller copies of the shape inside each other, each step its own colour. Steps can shrink by Scaling, by Even band (every step equally thick, following each bend) or by Blend (steps round off into the centre point). The copies can be another shape from your library, and Morph into it changes them into a picked shape step by step. The centre point is a handle on the shape: drag it with any tool, right-click it to put it back in the middle.

• Inside fill Tiles: another shape repeated like wallpaper inside (tile size, gaps, grid or bricks, flips, tile colours, only whole tiles). Drag the centre point to slide them.

• Copy along a path: Speed Custom lets you draw a graph, so the copies bunch up or spread out along the path.

• Hz bass: Height and Random height effect lines, and Height knobs in the synth window. Over 100 % the Hz bass grows onto more keys (funnels only shrink); quiet keys leave gaps. Random height stops at 400 %.

• Hz shape: one main line and one main note length. A layer you change gets its own (Use for all layers / Back to the main shape), and Reset puts the length back too.

• Picking an SFZ soundfont warns about missing or unreadable sample files.

• Domino row: the Paste button is back (pastes notes copied in Domino at the play line). Export and Copy to Domino cut notes longer than 65,535 ticks and say so.

• Masks are easier to see, and several Invert masks on one shape now cut all their holes together.

• Spiderweb.exe saves its speed-ups after the first start, so later starts are quick.

• Faster: zooming, panning and letting go of huge shapes (about 3x), Hz bass notes and layers, Nested copies and their centre point, and opening and undoing in huge projects.

• Texts say "1 note", never "1 notes" or "note(s)".

• Fixes: every shape can be moved down to the bottom key; the box round pasted notes stays round them; Gate sensitive merge no longer changes note colours; Ctrl+Z in the image window no longer undoes the piano roll; one damaged saved colour list, pattern or rhythm no longer loses the rest; windows fit on small screens; Spiderweb starts on Linux with older Python; error logs no longer show your folder paths.

## Version 1.5.4

What's new:

• Hz shape: Save… keeps the line you drew under a name. Saved lines show in Shapes under the ready-made ones, newest first, and Delete takes one out.

• Every list of saved names (shapes, presets, formulas, palettes...) shows the ready-made ones first, then a divider, then yours newest first. Typing a letter jumps to the first name starting with it.

• Error and crash logs now also say which PC, screen, speed-ups, MIDI out, soundfont (name and size), Built-in BASSMIDI and Hz preview settings were used, so problems are easier to track down. Only file names are written, never folders.

• Fixed the Hz bass preview crash: the soundfont player could throw away samples while another part was still loading them (use after free). It happened mostly with big .sfz soundfonts.

• Fixes: in Hz bass chords, a note cut by another tone's hit keeps its longer gate in the next note; Hz shape keeps its line on an Hz bass with no notes yet and after undoing the first note, and dragging stays smooth with many keys.

## Version 1.5.3

What's new:

• Hz bass: Wave shape… is now Hz shape…, and Note length moved into it. Note length can be a % of a wave (the new first choice), so short notes trace the drawn line on every pitch; a fraction like 1/8 works too. Faint bars show each note's length (drag a bar's end to change them all), and small strips show how the notes look at low and high pitches.

• Hz bass preview: picking another Hz bass puts the play line back at its first note, and the sound is made half a second after your changes settle (Space makes it at once).

• Error logs are now spiderweb-error.log and spiderweb-crash.log, right next to Spiderweb. If Spiderweb ever closes by itself, spiderweb-crash.log says where it happened: please include it when you report a problem.

• Fixes: Spiderweb could close by itself while an Hz bass preview played after picking another Hz bass.

## Version 1.5.2

What's new:

• Sustain: each note can be held to a set length at most, or stopped every so many ticks (the sustain starts again with no gap). You can also put stops by hand: they show as lines on the piano roll that you can drag, add and remove. Lengths in ticks or beats, and the piano roll stays usable while the window is open.

• Hz bass works on text: one tone for all the letters, and the text keeps its length.

• Chop and Slice are no longer beta.

• Help pages brought up to date, with a new page listing every shape.

• Fixes: Hz bass notes stay on their beats when a shape's left side is dragged or its text is retyped; text erased and typed again keeps its settings; a shape fits its notes again after its box is changed.

## Version 1.5.1

Before you update:

• Spiderweb's own files (autosave, presets, shapes, palettes, errors.log...) now live in one "user" folder. They are moved there by themselves on the first start.

• Notes shapes that were turned, slanted or stretched taller are now made like a turned image: no gaps or staircases, so they can come out a little different.

• Ctrl+Shift+V and the Paste button in the Domino row are gone: Ctrl+V pastes notes copied in Domino.

What's new:

• Settings (gear button, top right): Look (dark mode, size, font, text size, button style, edges, shape line thickness, with a live preview), note colour palettes you can make and switch, Sound, General (updates, tips) and Language.

• Translations: Spiderweb can be translated. Settings → Language → Start a translation.

• Masks: tick Mask (M) and draw a shape: the shapes under it show only inside it. Invert makes a hole, masks can be combined (Add / Subtract / Intersect), and Apply mask makes a new shape of exactly what you see.

• Copy along a path: right-click a shape → Copy along a path…. Copies it to a point along a line, curve, arc or formula, with size, speed, colours and flips changing along the way. Turn into plain shapes makes each copy its own shape.

• Sustain: right-click → Sustain…. Holds notes on each key until the end of their group, for players that lag.

• Gate aware flip: right-click → Gate aware flip. Every key row of a spam shape is reversed exactly, gates and velocities included.

• Use channel 10: a tick under Multi channel lets shapes use all 16 channels.

• 31-EDO: at 256 keys, a 31-EDO tick shows the piano roll in 31 notes an octave, and Audio plays it that way with Built-in BASSMIDI.

• Cut with Ctrl+X (shapes, drawer strokes, Hz bass notes and effect points). Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes too.

• Colours: Diagonal, each key row starts a few channels later.

• Clicking a selected shape again picks the next shape under it (a drag still moves it).

• Gate sensitive merge is no longer experimental.

• Faster: Fill / Spam with many lines up to 3x, area colours about 2x, placing slow custom shapes.

• Slice tool: cuts pasted notes by time, only on the key rows it crosses.

• The Settings… buttons are now Sound settings… (under MIDI out) and Hz settings… (Hz bass window).

• Many fixes.

## Version 1.5.0

Before you update: a lot changed inside Spiderweb, so a project or shape made in 1.4.0 can come out a little different. Keep a copy of your projects, your autosave and your shapes folder, and keep the old Spiderweb.exe for a while so you can compare. A project saved in 1.5.0 may not open fully in 1.4.0.

What can be different in old projects:

• Hz bass: the sound engine was rebuilt so every knob and effect line works, and the sound runs on through slides and glides. An old Hz bass can sound a little different. "Mixed" gates are now called Alternating.

• Fill, Spam and coloured areas: stray notes along lines that end on other lines, thin tips and colour borders are gone, so some shapes get slightly different notes there.

• Turned or slanted shapes with Claw, Strum or Chop: pieces that touch on a key now join into one note.

• Spam gates are whole ticks (a typed fraction is rounded). Pattern loops stop at 50,000.

• Turn into live shape moved from Ctrl+L to Ctrl+R.

What's new:

• Image tool (I): turns an image into notes in up to 15 colours, one per channel. Set it up in its window (size, colours, blending, how it looks in your player), then drag it into the piano roll.

• Claw machine, Strum and Chop are now pages of one window (Alt+W, Alt+S, Alt+U). Chop (beta) cuts every note into a rhythm; Ctrl+U chops at the snap length.

• Slice tool (K, beta): drag a line across shapes to cut them.

• Glue: right-click → Glue touching notes. On each key, notes that touch become one long note.

• Add between: select two shapes, right-click → Add between…. It makes the shapes that step from one to the other, with a graph for how the steps change.

• Gate sensitive merge (experimental): select two shapes, right-click → Gate sensitive merge. Each key row of one shape slides over until it meets the other shape's notes.

• Spam gate Range…: the gate can change across the shape, smoothly or in Steps, and each key row can have its own. Funnels have it too.

• Funnels can be a Hz bass, and the Slice tool cuts funnels.

• Hz bass synth window (Synth… in the Hz bass window): knobs, presets, effects, macros, a second oscillator, an arpeggiator and a MOD tab, like a real synth. Play it live from your computer keyboard.

• Hz bass layers: each layer has its own notes and its own sound, with mute and solo. The Loudness pane draws how loud each note and each layer is.

• More in the Hz bass window: drawing tools (Line, Polyline, Freehand, Curve, Arc) turn a path into notes and slides; the Bend tool (B) bends a note on its own; slides can curve; Wave shape… and Note length are in Settings…; notes copied in Domino paste in with Ctrl+V.

• Custom shape drawer: the Eraser drags a box, the Slice tool and Split here cut strokes, Mirror draws both sides at once, and a Layers list holds the strokes (hide, lock, groups). Points stick to other strokes' points, crossings and lines.

• Dark mode: Help → About → Look. Restart Spiderweb to see it.

• Playback: Built-in BASSMIDI (Spiderweb's own synth with your soundfont) and OmniMIDI (KDMAPI) are in the MIDI out list.

• Export to Domino: saves every note as a Domino song file (.dms).

• Toolbar: the drawing tools are one button with a ▾ list. Pin a tool there to give it its own button.

• Much faster with lots of notes: zooming, scrolling, moving shapes and letting go. Spiderweb offers to install Numba and Pillow (optional) to go faster still.

• Linux: Spiderweb should run on Linux too. Nobody has tested it there yet, so please report how it goes.

## Version 1.4.0

• Hz bass tool (H): spam so fast it sounds like a tone. Drag where it starts on the piano roll, then place notes in the window that opens (chords, slides). The red line there is the tone you really hear. Preview plays the Hz bass alone through a built-in synth with your soundfont.

• Hz bass effects: lines drawn under the notes change the sound over time (pitch, volume, vibrato, wah, tremolo and more). They can repeat, for example for a pumping bass.

• Select box: drag on empty space with Select to select what the box touches. Ctrl+drag adds another box. Drag a box's sides to stretch what it selected, the inside to move it, Ctrl+drag inside to move a copy. Right-click inside shows what works on all of them at once.

• With Select, double-click on empty space to paste there.

• Sharing shapes: Ctrl+C also copies the shapes as one line of text. Paste it anywhere to share it; others paste it into their Spiderweb with Ctrl+V. In the drawer, Export and Import do the same for drawings (Import adds them to your shape library).

• Scrollbars on the piano roll: drag the bar to scroll, drag one of its ends to zoom, or use the − / + buttons.

• Coloured areas: in the drawer, the new Areas tool (B) gives each piece of a custom shape a colour of its own, or leaves it empty. Click an area or drag across several; "Reset all colours" puts them all back. A line under the colours counts them (a shape has at most 15). On the piano roll they count with Fill or Spam and Multi channel.

• Stroke roles (right-click a stroke): Outline and fill (normal), Outline only (notes on top of the fill, cuts no hole), or Fill line (no notes, just splits the inside into areas to colour).

• Outline colour (right-click a stroke): the stroke's notes in a colour of their own. "Outline between colours" (under Fill and Spam, with Outline on) puts the notes where two colours meet on the outline too.

• Colours (side panel, under Velocity): any shape's notes take turns over several channels by step, key or time, so the colour keeps changing.

• Smoother with lots of notes: moving the view and dragging shapes.

• New pattern: Curls (right-click a line → Formula → Pattern along the line). The line loops back over itself in little curls; "curl" sets how tight they are. In Custom…, the new "along =" formula moves a pattern forward or back along the line.

• Formulas on more lines: polylines have them now, and so do the strokes of a live shape on the piano roll and the drawer's lines, polylines and arcs (right-click the stroke → Formula). Turn into live shape (Ctrl+L) keeps them.

• Strum: select shapes, right-click → Strum… (Alt+S). The notes of each chord start one after another, like strumming a guitar, so a straight edge of notes becomes a slope or a curve. It works like the strum tool of other piano rolls (Time, Velocity, Tension, Preserve end, Trigger ahead, End, Chop chords, Alternate direction), and every knob shows its real value, which you can type past where the knob reaches. The shape remembers its strum.

• Claw machine: no longer beta. Its shortcut is now Alt+W (was Ctrl+K).

• Outline gate (side panel, with Empty, Outline spam or Outline on): makes the outline at least so many ticks thick, so it doesn't get thin where the shape is steep. It grows into the inside, so the shape's edge stays where it is.

• The tumour window works like the Claw machine and Strum windows: changes show at once, Accept keeps them (one undo step), X or Esc puts the lines back, and Reset puts every setting back.

• Selected shapes keep their own colours: a thin red line goes round their notes instead.

• The side panel only shows the settings that do something for what's picked.

• Join, Split and Turn into live shape left the Shapes box: they're in the right-click menu and on Ctrl+G, Ctrl+Shift+G and Ctrl+L.

## Version 1.3.0

• Polygon tool (Q), next to Circle, instead of Square and Triangle: pick how many points it has and its kind in the panel (polygon, star, or crossing star). Its sides can have a shape or a pattern (right-click → Formula).

• Claw machine: select shapes, right-click → Claw machine… (Alt+W). It cuts slices of time out of the notes, or keeps some notes and trashes some (by notes, keys, chords or at random), and can shorten notes instead of removing them. The shape remembers its claw, so moving or reshaping it keeps it.

• Formulas: right-click a curve, line or arc → Formula. "Shape of the line" makes it a circle, spiral, flower, heart, figure 8, slow or fast start, or S-curve. "Pattern along the line" lays a wave, zigzag, bounce, square wave, sawtooth, teeth, spikes, half circles, stair steps, swell or fading wave along it. Custom… makes your own, with a preview you can zoom and move. Drawer curves, funnel curves and polygon sides have them too. "Turn into plain curve" makes the result an ordinary curve.

• Symmetric halves (Off / Mirrored / Turned) for formula shapes and patterns: the first half makes the second. Shapes changed by hand or turned into a plain curve stay symmetric.

• Velocity pane: a Formula tool (a line with a wave, zigzag, bounce or saved pattern swinging around it).

• Update check: Spiderweb can look for a new version when it starts (every time, once a day, week or month, or never) and show what's new in it. Set it in Help → About; "Check now" looks right away. Nothing is downloaded by itself.

• This What's new page.

• Undo and Redo buttons on the toolbar (between Fit view and Play).

• Funnel: lines on both sides of the wall make two halves, each with its own notes running to the wall. Notes ending on the wall make the same steps as notes starting on it.

• Paste from Domino with Multi channel: every copied track gets a channel of its own.

• Easier picking of lines, strokes, funnel parts and a box's corners and sides. Clicking the faint dashed line under a formula curve selects the shape.

• Steep lines give 1-tick notes to keys that share a tick.

• The Custom… formula window and the tumour graph have their own undo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y). The graph has From / to boxes and a right-click menu on its points.

• Curves fitted from drawn paths follow long, wiggly paths with fewer anchors, and sharp corners stay sharp.
