What changed in each version, newest first. This page opens by itself the first time a new version starts.

## Version 1.8.0

What's new:

• Spiderweb is now a folder: a small Spiderweb.exe with the program in a data folder beside it. Nothing is unpacked or written when it starts any more, so it starts much faster, most of all from a hard drive (a later start about 3 s → 0.7 s), and the copy of the whole program it used to unpack at every start is gone. Updating from 1.7 with "Update and restart" moves you over by itself; your files stay. The download is now a zip: unzip it and run Spiderweb.exe. See [page:about#Program files|About].

• Repair: Spiderweb.exe checks its program files at every start, and when some are missing or damaged (or the last start crashed) it offers to download this version again and put back only those files. Help → About → Check program files compares every file with its fingerprint.

• Add between works with drawn shapes, freehand lines, and a drawn shape with a line: each piece turns into the nearest piece of the other shape, and pieces with no partner grow or shrink along the way. A turned copy makes steps that turn instead of melting, and the new Turn by box turns or spins them by any angle. See [page:between#Drawn shapes|Add between].

• Copy along a path can turn the copies: the new Turn boxes under Size set the first and the last copy's angle, and the ones between turn step by step (0 → 360 spins them once along the path). Bigger, smaller and turned copies are now made again from your drawing, so their edges stay clean instead of being stretched into steps. See [page:path|Copy along a path].

• Settings → Piano roll → Even key rows: every key row the same number of pixels tall, in the piano roll and the Hz bass window. Zooming up and down snaps to whole row heights, and Fit view picks the biggest one where every key fits.

• Faster: editing a project with big drawn shapes, like Add between with hundreds of drawn steps, no longer slows down with their size (about 4 to 5 times faster with 500 steps).

• Faster: the outline of any drawn shape makes its notes much faster, so Copy along a path's grown and turned copies of a drawing are made about twice as fast.

• Faster: long tempo ramps at a high PPQ get ready to play and export several times faster (a 400-beat ramp stepping every tick at PPQ 15360: 0.8 s → 0.13 s).

• Faster: very long curved catch-ups get ready about 10 times faster (200,000 beats at PPQ 32767: 6.6 s → 0.65 s), exporting huge Keep timing parts takes about a third to a quarter of the time, and long formula tempo ramps work out about 6 times faster.

• Faster: Fit bar lines works through about a million bars a second (before: a few thousand). It now fits the first 67,108,864 bars of a song (before, a song past about a million bars got no fitting at all, even near its start), and the export says where it stops.

• Faster: Keep timing exports of long or busy songs use about 60 to 70 % less memory and take up to 40 % less time (16 million notes: 14.8 s and 5.5 GB → 8.4 s and 2.2 GB). The file comes out the same.

• Fixed: the export's "notes in a jump play off time" warning went missing when the song also had a Keep timing part.

• Fixed: Export MIDI didn't ask about silences too long for one step when a Keep timing part stretched them in the file, and on long songs with thousands of tracks that check made the window hang for seconds.

• Fixed: a Keep timing part drawn far faster than the tempo heard before it (like 60,000,000 BPM over a part heard at 125) could make Export MIDI run out of memory.

• Faster: Export MIDI of very big projects is about a tenth quicker (32 million notes: 1.6 s → 1.45 s).

• Faster: Load MIDI reads big MIDI files faster, most of all files with a few huge tracks, and uses half of the computer's threads, so other programs keep running smoothly on smaller PCs.

• Faster: drawn shapes with many coloured areas make their notes up to 3 to 4 times faster, so projects holding them open sooner, and an edit's notes made in the background no longer wait for older ones that aren't wanted any more.

• Smaller: a loaded MIDI's saved index now takes about 16 times less disk space, and Kestrel's kept read-ahead of its busiest parts about 17 times less (3 GB becomes under 200 MB on a very dense MIDI). On PCs with several cores the read-ahead is kept several times faster, and the index saves a bit faster. Indexes saved before keep working; delete one in Settings and save it again to shrink it.

• Lighter: Kestrel (GPU)'s read-ahead of a loaded MIDI's busiest parts takes about a fifth less memory while playing, and plays exactly the same.

• Fixed: Kestrel (GPU)'s read-ahead of a loaded MIDI's busiest parts was never offered for keeping, so it was read again at every load.

• Fixed: loaded MIDIs with huge tick numbers (like songs at 60,000,000 BPM with PPQ 32767) now load, show, play on every MIDI out and export properly. Before, Kestrel (GPU) couldn't start on them, playback fell far behind, zooming out froze, tempo changes far into the song were lost, and exporting with a changed tempo could fill the computer's memory. Tempo ramps there keep their exact Interval steps, and catch-ups and Keep timing parts work at any length (very long curved ones change in slightly longer steps, and the export message says so). Tick numbers up to 4,503,599,627,370,496 (2^52) now count as a normal MIDI, and notes far into such songs play exactly on time even at slow tempos, where they used to come up to about 70 ms off.

• Smoother: panning and dragging near a selected shape with many curves (its lines are drawn the quicker way, and its curves aren't worked out again at every step).

• While notes are being made after you turn or slant a shape, it shows only its outline, not its old unturned notes.

• Fixed: a drawn shape coloured by areas and bent by a Stir, Warp or Liquify set to Shape (with "Colours bend too" ticked) got stray notes of a neighbouring colour along its colour borders.

• Fixed: some damaged MIDI files stopped Load MIDI with an error instead of loading.

• Fixed: saving and opening a project could change its notes. Dragged points and velocity lines are now saved exactly (before, a few notes could move or get 1 louder after opening), and stirred, sliced and merged pieces keep their colours, step order and velocities. Glue in a box on a sliced piece no longer changes velocities outside the box.

• Fixed small rounding mistakes that changed the notes: Notes by snap could add an extra note one step early on the last key; Wrapped slices could give one tick two different slices; Hz bass could add an extra wave after a held tone; Gate Range across the keys could miss its first or last gate; a velocity line could read a stretched paste's note a tick off.

• Fixed small rounding mistakes in the tools: boxes with an edge between two ticks (a fine snap at a low PPQ) could leave a note out; Bend → Wobble could end above or below the note; Turn in the drawer landed off the grid on grids 12, 24 and 48; pasting Hz effect points onto a repeat's start did nothing; the Conductor's Interval box and the Hz Repeat every box cut a typed fraction down (2.9 became 2) instead of refusing it.

• Fixed: in Copy along a path, a grown copy could reach a key row or two past the point it should end on, and copies past the top key or before bar 1 weren't kept with Keep notes made there on.

• Fixed: a funnel whose wall comes before its line start in time (flipped sideways, or turned past upright) lost pieces where a key row crosses it twice: holes, horns floating apart or missing. Such funnels in older projects get those pieces back.

• Gate trim is now called Trim shape, and it has a second mode: Wrapped slices. It cuts a shape's notes into curved slices as if the shape were wrapped round a ball, each slice its own colour, so a circle becomes a striped beach ball. Pick the number of slices and colours, the angle (top and bottom, sideways or slanted), and how round the slices look. Any shape works, and each slice can become a shape of its own. See [page:trim|Trim shape].

• Draw on Domino: while a shape waits on Domino (before it goes in), your drawing tool works like Select on it. Drag its line or inside to move it, drag its points or a curve's handles, drag its box corners and sides to resize it, or just outside a corner to turn it. Press anywhere else to draw a new shape; hold Ctrl to draw even on top of it. The pointer shows the move arrows where a press changes the shape. See [page:companion|Draw on Domino].

• Fixed: Draw on Domino flashed black and stuttered while you resized Domino's panes (dragging the event list wider, for example).

• Draw on Domino: while you pick Glissando keys, the row of the key you hold gets a faint shade.

• Fixed in Draw on Domino: Spiderweb's own Select box no longer shows over Domino; the drawing layer is no longer tiny before Domino's grid is found; undo no longer loses track of lines after pasting, panning or pressing keys; Ctrl+Y after the last redo no longer goes on into Domino.

• Fixed: Rendered ahead went silent on songs with many tempo jumps, could fail on Merlin with long notes at very fast tempos, and on Built-in BASSMIDI cut a note short after a tiny note on the same key. Its pieces also take less memory while they're made.

• Fixed: the Hz preview on Built-in BASSMIDI now follows the real tempo, and the Hz window's first held note shows the right tempo's lines.

• Gate extend and Gate subtract: type the amount the way you mean it. 120 = ticks, 50% = half of each note's length, 50% + 10 = both, and math like 960*2 works. Gate subtract can use % now too. Amount in follows what you type. See [page:extend#The amount|Gate extend].

• Your own defaults: the pin in the tool windows (Gate extend and subtract, Sustain, Spam gate range, Tumours, Claw machine, Strum, Chop, Trim shape, Copy along a path, Shares, Stir, Warp, Liquify, Fill with tempo jumps, Channel per strip, Add between, Image to notes) saves the window's settings as what shapes start with. Settings → [page:settings#User set defaults|User set defaults] lists them, changes them and resets them, and can hide the pins.

• Warp and Liquify no longer carry the last picks over to the next shape: every opening starts from the program's own, or from your pinned default.

• The pins in the drawing tools list now point down when pinned and lean when not.

• Every [page:numbers|number box] can now be dragged sideways by the box itself, and its name still drags it too. Start / end pairs move together without losing their gap: drag Velocity's name or its →, Melody's BPM words, or the new ↔ between From and To in the Range and graph windows.

• One way of using the mouse and keys everywhere (new Help page: [page:controls|Mouse and keys]). Right-clicking a point in a graph, the Hz bass window or Copy along a path opens a menu with Delete point instead of deleting it at once, and the first and last points of a graph stay. Double-click never deletes a point (Sketch points included). Middle-click puts the Image sliders, the Trim slider and Add between's push pad back to their default. The note tool windows' Accept button is now OK, Enter presses the main button in Paste, Paste formula, Deform and Image, and F1 opens each tool window's own Help page. The LFO window's phase ◀ ▶ buttons turned around to match: Shift = a quarter, Ctrl = 1/64.

• Fixed: a double-click followed by one more quick click counted as a second double-click (a Sketch point vanished, quick Pencil clicks in the Hz window deleted the notes they placed); and the click that closed a graph's right-click menu added a point. Sustain stop lines and the Hz Effects pane's points now delete from their right-click menu too, and middle-click puts Stir's Centre point back in the middle.

• Arrow keys move the selected shapes: Left / Right one tick, Up / Down one key (the selected notes in the Hz bass window too). Quick presses in a row are one undo step. Home / End move the play line to the start / the last note, and Settings → Sound → [page:settings#When playback stops|When playback stops] picks where it goes when you stop.

• Files: File → Open Recent lists your last 16 projects and 16 loaded MIDI files. Open and Load MIDI start in the folder you used last. Each Save keeps a backup of the old file (5 per project, 1 GB in all: both in Settings → General), and File → Open a Backup… opens one. Exporting again over a MIDI file Spiderweb wrote no longer asks to replace it. See [page:files|Files].

• The Tools menu lists every tool, with the gate tools in their own Gate tools ▸ part. The Lasso and Slice buttons show which way they work. While the Liquify window is open, [ and ] change its brush size wherever you are. Backspace takes the last point off a polyline or arc you're still drawing (piano roll and drawer).

• The velocity pane has − and + buttons: every selected shape 1 quieter or louder (hold to keep going, one undo step). See [page:velocity|Velocity].

• A greyed control's help now starts with why it's greyed and what to do.

• Shape pictures: while the Shape list is open, a small picture of the highlighted shape shows beside it, and the drawer's shape library shows the shape under the mouse. Ctrl+click or Shift+click picks several shapes in the library to delete them together. The drawer's Save without a name asks for one ("Shape 1" filled in), and Ctrl+A there picks every stroke.

• Shape library folders: the drawer's shape library can hold folders (real folders in the shapes folder). Make, rename and delete them there, drag shapes in or use right-click → Move to, and every shape list (the Shape box, Nested, Tiles, Stir and Deform) groups your shapes under folder headings. Deleted shapes, folders and palettes now go to the Recycle Bin. See [page:drawer|Drawer].

• Every list of your own saved things keeps one file per item in the user folder's presets folder: Hz bass, Stir and Warp presets, Chop rhythms, saved curve patterns and shapes, Hz shape lines, wobble waves and colour formats. Deleting one sends it to the Recycle Bin. The old one-file lists are split up the first time each list opens, then renamed with "-old" at the end. Files you add or remove there show up the next time the list opens. The Custom formula window's saved list now shows the newest first.

• Fixed: shape pictures cut corners (a stray line across busy drawings) and left out detail. They now show every line, the colours you painted, and the shape's whole name.

• Drawer: Tracing picture… (beside Deform…) shows a picture faintly under the board to draw over, with its strength and Move / size. It's never saved in the shape. On Windows you can also drop a picture file onto the Image to notes window. See [page:drawer|Drawer].

• Drawer: Deform… has Copies, in its own box under Bends. At 2 or more, the selected strokes stay and turned copies are added, each turned a bit more: one line with Turn 22.5 and Copies 16 becomes the spokes of a wheel. The area has Position boxes, so you can type where its middle goes (50 / 50 = the board's middle). The drawer's Select tool also uses the piano roll's Select pointer now. See [page:drawer_deform|Deform].

• Stir tabs set to Shape have their own At the edges: Cut off (notes past the box cut off at its sides), Fit inside (the bent shape shrunk back into its box, keeping its proportions or each way on its own), Bounce back and Wrap around. Each tab does it right after its own bend. See [page:deform|Deform shapes].

• Drawer: Deform… has Cut off outside the area: whatever the bend pushes past the area's line is cut away, so a line turned inside a square stays inside it. The area, its handles and the Centre now snap to the grid while dragged (Shift = free), and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y work in the drawer while Deform is open. See [page:drawer_deform|Deform].

• Fixed: drawings with many lines meeting in one spot (like the spokes of a wheel) made the drawer and custom shapes slow, up to many seconds per change.

• Right-click → Turn → Turn by… turns shapes by a typed angle (80, 22.5, decimals too), live as you type. The right-click menus now keep their flips under Flip and their turns under Turn, on the piano roll and in the drawer (where Turn by… opens Deform). See [page:custom_edit].

• Warp: Save… beside Preset keeps a shape's bend as your own preset, at the top of the Preset list, for any shape in any project. See [page:warp|Warp].

• Hz bass window: right-click notes → Move to layer, and Duplicate layer in a layer's menu. The note count in its status line is now exact and updates after every change. See [page:hz_layers|Layers].

• Fixed: an open dropdown list stayed open, and could still be clicked, after you switched to another program.

• The settings backups made when you update now go into the user folder's backups\settings folder (older ones are moved there).

• Before you update: saved indexes of loaded MIDI files are made again, so the first load of each big MIDI after updating takes its full time once (and offers Save Index again). Projects with a loaded MIDI still find their file. A project with a loaded MIDI saved in this version and opened in an older one says its MIDI file changed, even though it didn't.

• Before you update: shapes with Gate Range across the keys (on a frame on the key lines) now run from the first gate on their lowest key to the last on their highest, so their gates can change a little. A few Hz bass notes, Notes by snap lines and Wrapped slices in older projects can lose an extra note or change one tick's colour where the rounding fixes above apply. Chop with step Off now keeps rhythm pieces shorter than a tick as 1-tick notes (they used to vanish, e.g. "half" left only every other tick), and Add between steps whose blended Gate subtract lands on half a tick now round up, like Gate extend.

• Before you update: Gate extend's box now reads a plain number as ticks. Type 50% for percent (it used to follow the Ticks / % choice). Projects open with the same notes as before.

## Version 1.7.5
^^ 2026/11/20 04:37:50 UTC+07:00 · 29438645

What's new:

• Fixed: the window sometimes froze for good after an edit (most often after Merge, Slice or Ctrl+Z) and had to be closed. It no longer does.
• Fixed: closed tool windows (Sustain, Chop, the drawer, the Hz bass window and others) stayed in memory, so memory use crept up during a long session.

• Thickness: a wide pen for every line tool (Line, Polyline, Freehand, Curve, Arc). Set Thickness in the side panel (or [ / ] while drawing; a circle around the mouse shows the size) and every key inside the band around your line gets one long note, like a ribbon. The pen's Ends can be round or flat, Taper thins both ends like a brush, and on Freehand, Width from speed makes quickly drawn parts thinner, like ink. The line itself stays thin. Slice, Join, Add between and Turn into live shape keep it. See [page:thickness|Thickness].

• Draw on Domino: a Settings button on the bar opens the shape settings (Velocity, Colours, Recolour, Deform…) in a small window over Domino, the same ones as in the side panel, so you can set Colours before drawing or change a shape before it goes in. See [page:companion#The Settings window|Draw on Domino].

• Notes kept past the keys and before bar 1 (Keep notes made there) now get real channels: they take part like notes inside the keys, as if the keyboard went on and the song started earlier, so every kept note shows the colour it would have inside, and Fold tracks, Fill gaps, Gate sensitive merge and Turn into plain notes copy them on those channels. The "Exact colours" setting is gone: the colours are always exact now. With Keep notes made there on, some shapes on the keys may get other colours than before. See [page:offrange|Notes past the keys].

• Opening a project file from someone else no longer changes your own setup: your tool settings for new shapes, Snap and the Hz bass window's view stay as they were. The file's song settings (PPQ, keys, channels...) still come with it.

• Settings → New projects: the PPQ, keys, channel settings, Domino start and output file that New and Load MIDI start with (before, New kept the open project's). Set them right there, or Use the open project's settings fills them in; untick Start new projects with these settings to work the old way. Fit view on New shows the whole empty project after New. See [page:settings#New projects:|Settings].

• Around the centre: a new Symmetric halves choice for a curve's Shape of the line. The shape is cut into equal slices round its centre (as many as a Flower's petals, or your own number) and only the first counts, so editing that one slice by hand in Custom… changes every petal, gear tooth or star point at once. Custom… shows the slice and the whole shape side by side; drag a point in the whole shape to change just that slice. See [page:around|Around the centre].

• Slice, Lasso and Split here: the pieces now take their notes straight from the shape they were cut from, made once as if it were never cut, so together they always hold exactly its notes. Before, each piece worked its notes out again, and with several effects stacked (Sustain, Gate steps, Gate aware flip, Stir, Warp, flips) a cut could lose a note, double one or change its velocity. A moved piece keeps its notes exactly; a piece you change afterwards keeps the shape's notes with only your change on top.

• While the Custom… formula window is open, the piano roll can now be panned with the middle button and scrolled with its scrollbars.

• Help → About Spiderweb opens the About page.

• The toolbar's "Live shape (G)" text turns light purple and "Mask (M)" light teal while ticked, so they're harder to leave on by mistake.

• Fixed: in Draw on Domino, dragging with the middle button did nothing while a drawing tool was picked. It now moves Domino's view as in Domino.
• Fixed: the side panel left a blank gap under the shape settings after picking a line and then a shape without point boxes.
• Fixed: a formula shape crossing over itself many times (like a Circle with "t from 0 to 30") froze the Custom… window for seconds. Long thick lines everywhere (drawer strokes, lasso, Conductor and velocity lines) are drawn quickly now.
• Fixed: the dot inside the Centre point ring (Stir, Clumps, Deform…) sat half a pixel off its middle.
• Fixed: the Hz bass window hid the bar number at the shape's start when the shape began a hair after the bar line.

• Fixed: with Built-in BASSMIDI, playing from the play line right on a note started that note a little late.
• Fixed: with Built-in BASSMIDI playing a loaded MIDI on several synths (Settings → Sound), the same note doubled on different channels sometimes sounded phased (hollow and swirly). Doubled notes now play exactly together.
• Fixed: with Built-in BASSMIDI, very loud dense walls made the sound dip and then creep back up for seconds (Windows' own volume limiter doing that). Spiderweb now cuts the sound at full volume itself, so it stays steady.
• Fixed: with Rendered ahead, notes held past the end of a rendered piece stopped there. They now keep playing to their end.

## Version 1.7.4
^^ 2026/11/18 15:03:55 UTC+07:00 · 0ada8871

What's new:

• Graphs on patterns: every number of a pattern along a line, Loops too, has a "…" button that opens a graph, so it can change along the line. The Loops graph makes the loops bunch up or spread out while their number stays the same; Height, Sharp, Waves and the others can grow or shrink toward the end (spikes getting taller and sharper, say). Works on lines, curves, arcs, live shape and drawer strokes, polygons and funnel curves; saved patterns keep their graphs. See [page:formulas#Graphs along the line|Formulas].

• Stir, Warp and Liquify can now bend the shape itself, not only its colours: pick "Moves: Shape" and the outline twists, swells, bends with the grid or follows the brush (a triangle whose middle swells up or caves in). Its notes bend like a picture, long notes cut into pieces where the bend curves them; untick "Cut notes where bent" to bend only the outline and lay the notes out again inside it. Pasted notes bend too. Shape bends run first, then every colour step, and they're never baked in. A Liquify set to Shape can push the shape out past its box. The three tools now sit on their own "Deform" row under Recolour, and Stir colours is now just "Stir". See [page:deform|Deform: Colours or Shape].

• Deform… in the custom shape drawer bends the drawing itself with Turn, Twist, Bulge / Pinch and Ripple, live on the board, round a Centre you drag. Like Stir, it can work inside an area (a circle, square, diamond or any library drawing) that you move, size, turn and slant right on the board. Curves stay curves, points stuck on lines stay on them, and coloured areas follow. See [page:drawer_deform|Drawer: Deform].

• Polygon: Twist bends every side into an S for a pinwheel swirl; a star's Inner turn swirls its inner points; Uneven makes the sides different lengths (Random, or your own lengths repeating round the shape); Points goes down to 2 (a line whose two sides a pattern pulls apart). A polygon's sides can also get a second pattern, on top of the first or taking turns side by side. See [page:box|Circle and Polygon].
• Flatten for patterns along a line: presses the bumps' tops flat like a balloon on a wall, with Softness for round or sharp edges. Patterns along lines are also made faster. See [page:formulas#Flatten|Formulas].
• Symmetrical steps: a tick at the top of a Spam shape's settings for perfectly even staircases. Every key row moves by the same whole number of gates, or one gate every few rows, so diagonals are even and mirrored halves match exactly. Drag freely: the notes show the even version and the corners snap there when you let go; unticking gives the drawing back. Nested bands become one row thick along flat sides and one gate along slopes, and with a gate Range (across time or keys) you pick Straight sides or Bend with the gates, where the sides curve along the gates in perfectly even steps. See [page:even_steps|Symmetrical steps].
• Nested now goes up to 480 steps (was 60) and Ripples up to 480 rings (was 120). Even band, Scaling, Ripples and the Shortest piece box are many times faster with lots of steps, and every filled shape is made a little faster. See [page:nested_tiles|Nested and Tiles].
• Nested Blend is now exact: every ring's edge falls on the exact tick of each key row, also with the Round toward the centre slider. It works on very tall shapes, uses far less memory and follows the centre point faster. Projects with a Blend fill may look slightly different. The new Shortest piece box gives thin slivers the colour of the pieces beside them.
• Nested, Tiles and Ripples now use all 16 colours when Use channel 10 is ticked (they stopped at 15).
• Formulas: Paste formula… takes a formula copied from a website, a graphing site or a piece of code (LaTeX too) and finds its parts by itself, with a preview. Formulas typed or pasted as written on websites work too (3t, 2π, sin²…). Symbols… types math signs your keyboard doesn't have, a closed shape can be typed as Polar r(t), and Flower has a Sharpness box for pointed stars or fatter petals. See [page:formulas#Paste formula|Formulas].
• Stir presets: keep stirs you like (the shown tab, or all tabs) and use them again on any shape, in any project. A preset looks the same on a flipped or turned shape. Stir's Ripple height can also go below 0, for a wave starting the other way. See [page:stir#Presets|Stir].

• Big projects stay responsive: after a change, the notes are made in the background while you keep working. This now covers Delete, undo and redo, flips, resizing, the right-click menu, the side panel, and the Stir, Warp, Liquify, Properties, Settings, Gate Range, Gate subtract, Gate extend, Sustain, Shares and Strips windows. A small "Making notes…" box shows on the piano roll meanwhile, and a resized shape's old notes stay, faded, in its new box until the new ones arrive. A setting change on a shape that didn't move keeps the piano roll as it was, red selection line too, until then. When counting a change's notes takes long, the "lots of notes" question comes a moment later, and No takes the change back.

• Change visual time signature… can now be changed later: its mark becomes a purple line in the Conductor pane. Drag its ends to cover other bars, drag the line to move it, or right-click it to edit the numbers, remove it, or make it permanent. Pointing at it shows the tempo those bars had before. Copy and paste in the Conductor pane carries it too. See [page:rebar#Changing it later|Change visual time signature].

• Scrollbars on the piano roll: drag the bar to scroll, drag one of its ends to zoom, or use the − / + buttons.

• Coloured areas: in the drawer, the new Areas tool (B) gives each piece of a custom shape a colour of its own, or leaves it empty. Click an area or drag across several; "Reset all colours" puts them all back. A line under the colours counts them (a shape has at most 15). On the piano roll they count with Fill or Spam and Multi channel.

• Stroke roles (right-click a stroke): Outline and fill (normal), Outline only (notes on top of the fill, cuts no hole), or Fill line (no notes, just splits the inside into areas to colour).

• Outline colour (right-click a stroke): the stroke's notes in a colour of their own. "Outline between colours" (under Fill and Spam, with Outline on) puts the notes where two colours meet on the outline too.

• Colours (side panel, under Velocity): any shape's notes take turns over several channels by step, key or time, so the colour keeps changing.

• Smoother with lots of notes: moving the view and dragging shapes.

• New pattern: Curls (right-click a line → Formula → Pattern along the line). The line loops back over itself in little curls; "curl" sets how tight they are. In Custom…, the new "along =" formula moves a pattern forward or back along the line.

• Formulas on more lines: polylines have them now, and so do the strokes of a live shape on the piano roll and the drawer's lines, polylines and arcs (right-click the stroke → Formula). Turn into live shape (Ctrl+L) keeps them.

• Strum: select shapes, right-click → Strum… (Alt+S). The notes of each chord start one after another, like strumming a guitar, so a straight edge of notes becomes a slope or a curve. It works like the strum tool of other piano rolls (Time, Velocity, Tension, Preserve end, Trigger ahead, End, Chop chords, Alternate direction), and every knob shows its real value, which you can type past where the knob reaches. The shape remembers its strum.

• Claw machine: no longer beta. Its shortcut is now Alt+W (was Ctrl+K).

• Outline gate (side panel, with Empty, Outline spam or Outline on): makes the outline at least so many ticks thick, so it doesn't get thin where the shape is steep. It grows into the inside, so the shape's edge stays where it is.

• The tumour window works like the Claw machine and Strum windows: changes show at once, Accept keeps them (one undo step), X or Esc puts the lines back, and Reset puts every setting back.

• Selected shapes keep their own colours: a thin red line goes round their notes instead.

• The side panel only shows the settings that do something for what's picked.

• Join, Split and Turn into live shape left the Shapes box: they're in the right-click menu and on Ctrl+G, Ctrl+Shift+G and Ctrl+L.
