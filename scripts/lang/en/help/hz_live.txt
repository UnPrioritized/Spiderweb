In the Hz bass window, press Synth… (it turns Preview on). The synth window has four tabs (the big buttons at the top left): OSC (the knobs), FX (the effects), MOD (things that move knobs over time) and Draw (the effects' lines for one note, big), and a keyboard along the bottom.

Click and hold a key to hear the Hz bass of that key, as a note placed there would sound: its keys, gates and effects. Let go, and the falls after the sustain points play. Drag sideways to play other keys. Hold several letters (or letters and the mouse) to play keys together, as on a synth; keys pressed at the same moment start together, as a chord.

You can play with your computer's keyboard too, like a small piano: the middle row of letters A S D F G H J K are the white keys from C up, W E T Y U above them the black keys, and O L P ; go on past the next C.

Z and X move them an octave down or up; the keys they don't reach are greyed on the keyboard, and the line at the bottom says which they play. Hold a letter and turn a knob with the mouse to hear the change. The letters don't play while you type in a box.

Presets (next to the tabs) are ready-made sounds: click the name to pick one, or step through them with ◀ ▶. Save… keeps the sound you have as a preset of your own (every knob and line, not the notes), and Delete removes one of yours. A * after the name means you've changed the sound since picking it. Picking a preset can be undone like any change.

The meter at the top right shows how many notes a second the sound makes while one A1 is held: a higher note makes more, a lower one fewer, and Voices on Same keys, Echo, Reverb look-alike and Arpeggio add notes.

What you hear is NOT your MIDI. It's a quick copy made by Spiderweb: each different note is played once by the preview's synth with your soundfont, and copies of it are laid where the notes start. That's fast enough to play even a 128-key Hz bass live.

It sounds very close to the real thing with most soundfonts; a soundfont that changes its sound at random can't be copied exactly. Only notes you place in the Hz bass window go into your MIDI.

The first press after a change (another soundfont, new effects) waits a moment while the notes are recorded. The recordings are kept while Spiderweb is open; Hz settings… → Memory for live keys says how much memory they may take. When it's full, the ones used least are thrown away and recorded again when needed.
