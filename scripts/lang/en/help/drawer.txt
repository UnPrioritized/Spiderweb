The drawer (Custom shape → Drawer…) is where you draw shapes for your library. Its tools work like the piano roll's: Select (V), Slice (K) (see Drawer: Slice), Eraser (E), Line (L), Polyline (P), Freehand (F), Curve (C), Arc (A), Square (S) and Circle (O). Areas (B) colours the areas inside (see Drawer: Areas). Drag a stroke out, or click where it starts and click where it ends (right-click or Esc cancels).

Grid (top bar) sets how many grid squares the board has across (4 to 64). Clear removes everything drawn (Ctrl+Z brings it back). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes.

Points stick to other strokes when the mouse is near them: to their points first, then to where two lines cross, then anywhere along a line (a purple mark shows where). A point stuck to a line is exactly on it, so the outline counts as closed there. Away from lines, points snap to the grid. Shift draws freely.

Mirror (next to Grid): every new stroke gets a mirrored copy across the middle of the board, shown while you draw. Horizontal mirrors left ↔ right, Vertical top ↔ bottom, Both into all four corners. The board's grey side is where the copies go, and a blue-green line marks the middle. Points stick to that line (on the grid along it), and halves whose ends meet on it join up into one outline (a half heart becomes a whole closed heart). The copies are normal strokes: change them on their own afterwards.

Layers (bottom right) lists every stroke, the last drawn on top. Click a row to pick that stroke (Ctrl / Shift+click picks several); picking on the board picks the rows too. Drag rows up or down to change the order. Double-click a name (or F2) to rename it; an empty name gives it back its own. Del deletes the picked strokes.

The eye shows or hides a stroke: hidden strokes aren't drawn and are left out of the shape on the piano roll, but stay in the saved drawing (the drawer warns while any are hidden). Alt+click an eye to show only that stroke; Alt+click it again to show them all. The lock keeps a stroke from being picked, moved or erased on the board (other strokes still stick to it). Right-click a stroke (on the board or in the list) for Invert selection, Hide selected and Lock selected.

Groups: pick some strokes, right-click one (on the board or in Layers) > Group. The group gets a folder row (click its arrow to fold it away); its eye and lock work on all of its strokes, and double-clicking its name renames it.

Clicking a grouped stroke on the board picks the whole group, so it moves, flips and turns as one; Ctrl+click picks a single stroke. Drag rows into a group or out of it in Layers, or drag the group's row to move it all. Right-click > Ungroup takes the group apart (the strokes stay). Pasted copies aren't in a group.

Save keeps it in the library (the shapes folder inside the user folder next to Spiderweb); Use on the piano roll picks it for the Custom shape tool. On the piano roll it stretches to the box you drag.

Open (or double-click a name, or Enter) opens a saved shape from the library; New starts an empty drawing. Both ask first if the drawing has changes that aren't saved.

Rename (or F2, or click the picked name again) changes a saved shape's name; Delete removes it. While the list has the keyboard (its picked name is blue), Delete and Enter work on the list; click the board to give the keyboard back to the drawing (the picked name turns grey).

Export copies the whole drawing as a line of text to share; Import adds a shared drawing to your library (see Sharing shapes).

Lines and curves that meet end to end count as one outline, and so does a line whose ends land on another line or on itself. Closed: Empty, Fill and Spam all work. Gaps (red dots at the loose ends): Fill and Spam close each one with a straight line (see Inside fill).

Wheel = zoom, middle-drag = scroll, Reset view = back to the middle of the board.
