The ⚙ button at the top right of the toolbar opens Settings: how Spiderweb itself looks and sounds. These settings are kept for every project (in settings.ini in the user folder), not saved with a project, and Ctrl+Z doesn't take them back.

Look tab:

• Look: Light, Dark, or Follow Windows. Restart Spiderweb to see the new look.

• Note colours: the colours of the notes on the piano roll, one per MIDI channel (the numbers under them; channel 10's colour is used only when a project ticks Use channel 10). Default is built in and never changes; New… makes your own palette from the one in use, and Rename… / Delete work on yours. Switching palettes shows at once.

• Click a colour to change it (changing Default makes a copy first). Random picks soft colours, Gradient… blends from a first to a last colour.

• Size, Font and Text size: how big everything is, the font, and the text a little smaller or larger. The Preview beside them shows a pick at once; the rest of Spiderweb changes after a restart.

• Buttons: how buttons and boxes are drawn (System's own, Plain, Raised, Outlined, Flat), and Edges how thick their edges are. Reset to default puts size, font, text size, buttons and shape lines back.

• Shape lines: how thick shapes' lines are on the piano roll. This one shows at once.

Sound tab: the MIDI out used for playback, and with Built-in BASSMIDI its soundfont, voice limit, lowering it by itself when it can't keep up, and Use OmniMIDI Limiter. The Sound settings… button under MIDI out opens this tab.

General tab: how often to look for a new version (and Check now), tips on or off, and Show all tips again.

Language tab: the language of every text, shown after a restart. Start a translation saves a copy of the English texts to fill in.
