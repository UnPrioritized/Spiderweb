Every number box (not PPQ) can be changed without typing: drag the name next to it sideways, or press Up / Down in it (the mouse wheel too, after a click in it). Shift = big steps, Ctrl = small steps. A name over two boxes (Velocity) changes both. Boxes also take math: 960*4, 1920/3…

The mouse wheel changes a number box, dropdown or knob only after you click it. Over anything not clicked, the wheel scrolls the window instead, so nothing changes by accident while you scroll past.
